Standard vs No-Activation-Fee: which is cheaper?

A Standard evaluation is usually cheaper up front, but after you pass you pay a PA activation fee, and coupon codes don’t discount it. The No-Activation build usually costs more up front and has a $0 PA fee. We don’t publish a break-even table: we couldn’t verify current PA activation fees, and our Intraday No-Activation list prices look out of line. At checkout, compare the No-Activation price with the Standard price plus the PA activation fee shown for your size.

Per a search-index excerpt of Apex’s help center (Oct 2026), No-Activation evaluations are offered on Intraday 25K–150K and EOD 25K/50K, while our price data also lists EOD 100K/150K No-Activation prices. Check what checkout offers at your size.
