Apex Trader Funding Payout Rules (2026): Caps, the 50% Rule & the Ladder

Apex funded accounts (PAs) keep 100% of approved payouts, but each payout has to clear five qualifying days, a 50% consistency rule and a safety net, and each one is capped. Here is how the rules work at every size.

The short answer
  • Split: funded PAs keep 100% of approved payouts (no profit split).
  • Before each payout: 5 qualifying trading days (not necessarily consecutive) at a minimum daily profit of $100–$300 (Intraday) or $100–$350 (EOD).
  • 50% consistency: no single day may be 50% or more of the profit since your last payout. Going over isn’t a breach; the payout just waits.
  • Safety net: only profit above the drawdown + $100 can be withdrawn, and the minimum request is $500.
  • Caps: from $1,000–$2,500 on payout 1 to $1,000–$5,000 on payout 6 (25K–150K). A PA closes after its 6th payout.

The Apex 50% consistency rule, with a worked example

On a PA, no single trading day may be 50% or more of the profit made since your last payout. It applies only to funded accounts (there is no consistency rule in the evaluation), and going over is not a breach: you simply can’t request until the ratio drops.

Worked example · first payout on a 50K Intraday PA

Five days of +$800, +$450, +$1,000, +$600 and +$750. Each clears the $200 minimum, so all five qualify. Profit is $3,600 and the biggest day ($1,000) is 28% of it, under 50%.

The safety net on 50K is $2,100, so $1,500 is withdrawable. That equals the payout-1 cap of $1,500, so you request $1,500 and keep all of it.

Same $3,600, different shape: +$1,800, +$300, +$500, +$400, +$600. The $1,800 day is exactly 50%, so the payout waits until total profit is above $3,600 with no new bigger day. (Our arithmetic from Apex’s rules.)

Intraday vs EOD PA: what changes for payouts

  • Same: 100% split, every 5 trading days, 5 qualifying days, 50% consistency, $500 minimum, 6 payouts per PA, and the same payout-1 and payout-6 caps.
  • Different minimum daily profit†: from 50K up, EOD days must clear more ($250 / $300 / $350 vs $200 / $250 / $300 on Intraday).
  • Different middle of the ladder†: per the help-center excerpts, payouts 2–5 step up at different points (tables above).
  • Different daily loss limit: EOD has a $500–$2,000 DLL. Intraday has none in the evaluation and a scaling DLL once funded.

Apex legacy account payout rules

Legacy PAs

Apex brings back its legacy accounts (six sizes up to $300K) during sale periods. Per Apex’s help-center excerpt†, a legacy PA caps only its first 5 payouts, from $1,500 on 25K up to $3,500 on 300K, and from payout 6 there is no cap. We couldn’t read the full legacy table, so check the cap for your size in your dashboard.

How to request an Apex payout

  1. Count five qualifying days since your last payout, each at or above the minimum for your size and PA type.
  2. Check the 50% rule: your best day must be under 50% of the profit since your last payout.
  3. Check the safety net: only profit above the drawdown + $100 is withdrawable.
  4. Pick the amount: at least $500, and no more than the cap for this payout number.
  5. Submit it from your Apex dashboard. Remember the PA closes after its 6th payout.

Why Apex payouts get held or denied

  • Fewer than five qualifying days: days under the minimum daily profit don’t count.
  • One day is 50% or more of the profit since your last payout.
  • Not enough above the safety net to cover the $500 minimum.
  • Asking for more than the cap for that payout number.
  • Prohibited trading: news-only or straddle strategies and hedging are not allowed (trading through news with your normal strategy is), and positions must be flat by market close.
  • Too many accounts: the limit is 20 active PAs across a household, companies and connections, every PA type combined. Evaluations are unlimited.

Common questions

Apex Trader Funding: frequently asked questions

What are Apex Trader Funding’s payout rules?+

Funded PAs keep 100% of approved payouts, every 5 trading days. Each payout needs 5 qualifying days, a best day under 50% of the profit since the last payout, and profit above the safety net (drawdown + $100). The minimum is $500, each payout is capped, and a PA closes after 6 payouts. Confirm the details in your Apex dashboard.

What is the Apex 50% consistency rule?+

On a funded PA, no single day may be 50% or more of the profit made since your last payout. It isn’t a breach; your payout waits until more profit brings the best day under 50%. There is no consistency rule in the Apex evaluation.

What are the Apex payout rules for a 50K account?+

On a 50K PA: 5 qualifying days of $200+ (Intraday) or $250+ (EOD), a safety net of $2,100, a $500 minimum, and caps from $1,500 on payout 1 to $3,000 on payout 6. The qualifying-day amounts and the safety net are from Apex’s help center (Oct 2026); confirm them in your dashboard.

What is the Apex payout cap?+

Payout 1 is capped at $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 for 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K, rising to $1,000 / $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000 by payout 6. The steps in between differ slightly between Intraday and EOD PAs.

How many payouts can you take from an Apex PA?+

Six. After the 6th payout the PA closes. You can hold up to 20 active PAs across a household.

What is the Apex minimum payout?+

$500. Per Apex’s help center (Oct 2026), only profit above the safety net (drawdown + $100) can be withdrawn.

What are the Apex legacy account payout rules?+

Per Apex’s help center, legacy PAs cap only the first 5 payouts, from $1,500 on 25K up to $3,500 on 300K, with no cap from payout 6. Check the cap for your size in your dashboard.

How do you request a payout from Apex?+

Once you have 5 qualifying days, a best day under 50% and profit above the safety net, request an amount between $500 and your current cap from your Apex dashboard.

Does Apex take a profit split?+

No. Funded PAs keep 100% of approved payouts, according to our September and October 2026 audits. Payouts are capped per request instead.

How long do Apex payouts take?+

We have not verified a processing time with Apex. The figure on our payout tracker (about 4 hours) is a PropChamps estimate, not an Apex commitment.
