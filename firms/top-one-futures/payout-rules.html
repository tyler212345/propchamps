Top One Futures Payout Rules (2026): Elite Access, Elite Daily, Instant Sim & Ignite

Top One’s four paths all pay a flat 90%, and every sim-funded withdrawal is capped until you go live. What differs is the trigger: profitable days, a daily buffer, or a profit target. Here are the numbers for each path at every size.

The short answer
  • All four main paths pay 90/10 (the limited LaunchPad X-Daily Spark release pays 80/20), and every sim-funded withdrawal is capped until you go live.
  • Elite Access: request after 5 profitable days ($200–$350+) with profit above the buffer + $500 ($2,000–$5,000). $500 minimum, $1,000–$2,500 cap.
  • Elite Daily: request every 24 hours from day 1, keeping a $1,500–$4,500 buffer. $500 minimum, $750–$2,250 cap, no funded consistency rule.
  • Instant Sim Funded: no evaluation. Profit target of 6% for payout 1, then 5%, then 4%, with 20% consistency and a $1,000–$3,000 cap.
  • Ignite: no evaluation. 5% profit target per payout, 15% consistency, $250 minimum, $500–$2,000 cap.

How to request a Top One payout

  1. Meet the trigger: Elite Access, 5 profitable days and profit above the buffer + $500; Elite Daily, profit above the buffer; Instant Sim, the current profit target; Ignite, the 5% target.
  2. Check consistency: 40% (Elite Access), 20% (Instant Sim) or 15% (Ignite). Elite Daily funded has none.
  3. From the 2nd Elite payout, make sure at least half of the amount is new profit since your last payout.
  4. Request between the minimum and the cap for your size from your Top One dashboard.

Why Top One payouts get held or denied

  • Consistency over the limit for your plan (15% on Ignite is the tightest).
  • Profitable days below the size minimum on Elite Access ($200–$350).
  • Not enough above the buffer on Elite Access or Elite Daily.
  • Less than 50% new profit on the 2nd or later Elite payout.
  • A news trade within 2 minutes of high-impact news on a funded Elite account.
  • Ignite trading rules: an overnight hold, a bot, or a trade under 10 seconds.
  • Account rules: limits are per household and per category, accounts in a category must be the same size, copy trading is allowed only between the same plan type and size, and if anyone in the household is live no sim accounts are allowed. Other firms: how many prop firm accounts you can have.

Common questions

Top One Futures: frequently asked questions

What are Top One Futures’ payout rules?+

All four main paths pay 90/10 (LaunchPad X-Daily Spark: 80/20) and cap each sim-funded withdrawal. Elite Access pays after 5 profitable days above a buffer; Elite Daily pays every 24 hours above a buffer; Instant Sim Funded pays at a 6% → 5% → 4% profit target with 20% consistency; Ignite pays at a 5% target with 15% consistency.

What are the Top One Elite Access payout rules?+

You need 5 profitable days of $200 / $250 / $300 / $350+ and profit of $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,500 / $5,000 (the buffer + $500) for 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K, plus 40% funded consistency. The minimum is $500 and the cap is $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 per request.

What are the Top One Elite Daily payout rules?+

Once funded you can request every 24 hours from day 1, keeping a buffer of $1,500 / $2,500 / $3,500 / $4,500 in the account. The minimum is $500, the cap is $750 / $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,250 per request, and there is no funded consistency rule.

What are the Top One Ignite instant funding payout rules?+

Each payout needs a 5% profit target (recalculated after every payout) and 15% consistency, with no minimum days. The minimum request is $250 and the cap is $500 / $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000. No overnight holds or bots, and close by 4 PM ET.

Instant Sim Funded vs Ignite: what is the difference?+

Both skip the evaluation and pay 90/10. Instant Sim Funded costs more ($419–$939 list), has 20% consistency and caps of $1,000–$3,000. Ignite is cheaper ($218–$799 list) but has 15% consistency, caps of $500–$2,000, and intraday-only trading rules.

Does Top One Futures have a payout cap?+

Yes. Every sim-funded withdrawal is capped until you go live: Elite Access $1,000–$2,500, Elite Daily $750–$2,250, Instant Sim Funded $1,000–$3,000 and Ignite $500–$2,000 per request.

What is the Top One Futures activation fee?+

Elite Access has one: $139 / $189 / $259 / $359 after you pass the $39 evaluation ($69 / $94 / $129 / $179 on Elite Access Intraday). Elite Daily has no activation fee, and Instant Sim Funded and Ignite are one-time purchases. The limited LaunchPad X-Daily Spark release also charges a $189 activation fee.

What is the Top One daily progression rule?+

On the Elite plans, from the 2nd payout on, at least 50% of each payout must be new profit made since your previous payout.

How do you get a live account at Top One Futures?+

Elite Access accounts are reviewed for live after 3 payouts, and only one account moves live. Elite Daily usually moves live after 5 payouts, and Ignite is reviewed after 3 payouts.
