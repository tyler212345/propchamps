Markets

What Vest Capital trades: USDC perpetuals, not CME futures

Vest Capital is the funded-account program of Vest Markets, and it is not a futures prop firm. Every market is a USDC-margined perpetual contract on Vest’s own venue: no expiry, funding paid hourly and cross margin only. That includes ES-PERP and NQ-PERP, which track the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 index futures but are Vest’s own contracts, not CME futures.

What changes if you trade futures today

  • No CME contracts or futures platforms. You trade in Vest’s own web app, with TradingView charts and Market, Limit and Scale orders (Reduce Only, GTC, IOC, FOK, ALO, TP and SL). Vest’s FAQ and docs list no NinjaTrader, Tradovate, Rithmic or MT4/MT5 connection, and API trading is not allowed on funded accounts.
  • Sizes are in units, not contracts. Per Vest’s docs, 1 MES = 5 ES units, 1 ES = 50 units, 1 MNQ = 2 NQ units and 1 NQ = 20 units.
  • Funding every hour. Perps have no expiry or rollover, but funding payments settle hourly on top of trading fees. PnL and balances use the mid price; liquidations use the mark price.
  • Weekend locks. Non-crypto markets lock from Friday 8:00 PM ET, and leverage is cut from Friday 4:00 PM ET (see trading rules). Crypto trades 24/7.
  • Sign-up is by email, Google or a crypto wallet, with no username or password.

Loss limits

Vest Capital drawdown and daily loss limit

The max drawdown is static: a fixed floor set from your starting capital that never trails, checked intraday against account value including open losses. It is 2% on $500, 4% above on Instant, 6% on the 1-Step cards and the 2-Step, and 6% / 7% / 8% in the builder. A daily loss limit applies only to evaluation-path accounts that include one, and it resets at 8:00 PM ET.

What ends an account

A breach closes the account immediately and permanently: no warning, no grace period, no reset and no retry. You buy a new account instead. Both limits apply in the evaluation and once Live, overnight and over weekends. Trading fees are simulated in the evaluation but still count toward your drawdown and daily limit; once Live they are charged.

Where Vest’s own pages disagree

  • Touching the floor. Vest’s FAQ says touching or dropping below it breaches; its landing page says the account breaches when it “drops through it”; its docs say equality alone does not breach. Treat touching it as a breach.
  • The daily-loss base. Vest’s docs measure the limit from the 8:00 PM ET reset balance excluding open PnL, with equity checked against that floor continuously; its FAQ describes a check against equity at 8:00 PM ET.
  • Drawdown options. The FAQ says 6% on every evaluation-path account, but the builder sells 7% and 8%.

Instant: the fee is the whole loss allowance

On Instant the fee equals the drawdown, so $2,000 buys a $50K account that closes at $48,000 of account value, open losses included, with leverage of up to 75x on FX and ES perps. Claims don’t move the floor: only profit above starting capital can be claimed, so each claim brings your balance back toward it (our reading of Vest’s docs). On evaluation-path accounts a claim also lowers that day’s daily-loss floor by the amount claimed.

Trading rules

Leverage, weekend and trading rules

There is no consistency rule, no minimum number of trading days and no time limit. News trading, scalping and HFT-style trading are allowed, and you can hold overnight and over weekends. What you can’t do: trade through an API on any funded account, or keep full leverage into the weekend, when Vest cuts it from Friday 4:00 PM ET.

Weekends: market locks and Temporary Leverage Limits

  • Non-crypto positions lock from Friday 8:00 PM ET. ES, NQ and commodity perps reopen Sunday 6:00 PM ET, single stocks Sunday 8:00 PM ET. Crypto trades 24/7.
  • From Friday 4:00 PM ET until the reopen, index perps (ES, NQ, RTY, YM) and all 7 FX perps drop to 5x, and GC and SI to 3x.
  • Positions more than 2% above the reduced cap are cut down to it. If your account value is more than 1% below starting capital, those positions are closed in full instead.
  • Nothing is reduced if your account is not in drawdown and covers the margin the new limits require.

What is banned

  • API trading on any funded account: evaluation or Live, Instant included.
  • Abuse or coordination across funded accounts, such as opposite positions in the same market on different accounts, and collusion with other traders.
  • Under Vest’s Prop Trading Terms (§5): manipulating prices, funding rates or oracles; wash trading, spoofing and layering; front-running Vest’s hedges; multiple identities; exploiting latency, errors or payout calculations; and using a VPN to get around restrictions.
  • Third-party bots and trade copiers are not banned outright but are at your own risk (Prop Terms §9, from a reading we have not re-checked).

There is no per-trade position-size cap beyond the loss limits and margin.

Eligibility

Can US traders use Vest Capital? Unconfirmed

Unconfirmed, and we won’t say US traders can use it until Vest confirms that in writing. Vest’s Prop Trading Terms don’t list the US as a prohibited jurisdiction, but its Exchange Terms bar US persons, and your claimed profit lands in the Primary Account, which sits on the exchange side. If you are in the US, ask Vest support to confirm in writing that you can complete verification, receive a claim and withdraw it before you buy.

Account type

Is Vest Capital real capital? What the terms say

No, by Vest’s own binding terms. The Prop Trading Terms say every account, Instant included, is simulated: no real trading capital, margin or collateral is credited to you, and your fee buys access to a service. Payouts are compensation from Vest’s general corporate funds, which fees help pay, and Vest does not claim they come from profitable live trading. Its marketing says otherwise (“Our Capital. Your Profit.”, and “real capital” in its FAQ and docs), so we go by the terms.

The program’s legal footer says the same in other words: it uses virtual accounts to collect non-binding trade ideas, traders don’t trade live capital, and Vest may record a hypothetical result, or trade and hedge for its own account, on Vest Exchange, an affiliated venue or third-party venues.

That model is common: most futures prop firms’ funded accounts are simulated too, and payouts come from the firm. How prop firms make money. What sets Vest apart is that its marketing contradicts its own terms.

Conflicts of interest the terms disclose

  • Vest runs the simulator, so it controls fills, slippage, latency and the price impact of your own trades.
  • It may trade or hedge your ideas on its own affiliated exchange, and it keeps third-party rebates.
  • It promises not to worsen a trader’s fills selectively just because the trader is profitable (Prop Terms §6(e), §6A and §8).
  • The terms say they can change at any time without prior notice, while §6(r) promises reasonable advance notice of material changes and no retroactive denial of compensation already earned.

Limits

How many Vest Capital accounts can you have?

Up to 10 Live funded accounts at once, Instant accounts included, and up to 100 evaluation accounts. Refunds are possible only within 3 days of purchase and only if you haven’t placed a trade; a failed evaluation is not refundable.

  • Vest’s account-limit settings return 100 evaluation and 10 funded accounts. Its Prop Terms (§2) word it as up to 10 Instant accounts and 100 evaluation accounts per registration profile, while its FAQ and docs count every Live account toward the 10 and the FAQ says evaluation accounts aren’t capped.
  • At the largest size that is ten $50K accounts, about $500K in all (our arithmetic). There are no $100K or $150K accounts and no scaling plan, and we found no inactivity rule.
  • Refunds go back through Stripe in 5-10 business days.

Verdict

Our verdict on Vest Capital

Vest Capital is a low-cost, low-friction way to trade a funded-style account across crypto, stocks, FX, indices and commodities: one-time fees from $10, no consistency rule, no minimum days and claims whenever you want, paid in USDC. Weigh that against what it is not: a futures firm, a real-capital account under its own terms, or a confirmed option for US traders. It is also a young offshore operator with three pricing incidents in 2026.

It suits you if

  • you trade crypto, single stocks, FX or commodities as well as index perps and want one rule set for all of them
  • you want very small, cheap accounts: a $500 Instant for $10, or a 2-Step from $15
  • you dislike consistency rules, minimum days and payout buffers, and want to claim profit whenever you choose
  • you already use a self-custody crypto wallet and are happy to be paid in USDC
  • you live outside the US and the UK (or Vest has confirmed your eligibility in writing) and accept an offshore operator with arbitration in Panama

Look elsewhere if

  • you want CME futures on NinjaTrader, Tradovate or Rithmic: see the futures firms we rank
  • you are in the US or the UK and Vest hasn’t confirmed in writing that you can claim and withdraw
  • you need bank or fiat payouts, or accounts above $50K
  • you hold through big intraday swings: the floor counts open losses and sits only 4% (2% on $500) away on Instant
  • you run API or automated strategies on funded accounts
  • you want a long, independently verified payout record

FAQ

Vest Capital FAQ

Is Vest Capital a futures prop firm?+
No. Every Vest Capital market is a USDC-settled perpetual contract on Vest’s own venue: crypto trades 24/7, and stocks, ETFs, indices, FX and commodities 24/5, with funding paid hourly. Its ES and NQ markets are perps that track the index futures, not CME contracts, and it runs only on Vest’s web app (no NinjaTrader, Tradovate or Rithmic).
Can US residents use Vest Capital?+
Unconfirmed. Vest’s Prop Trading Terms don’t list the US as prohibited, but its Exchange Terms bar US persons, and claimed profit lands in the Primary Account, an exchange account that Vest’s site restricts for US IP addresses. If you are in the US, get written confirmation from Vest that you can verify, claim and withdraw before you buy.
Is Vest Capital real capital or simulated?+
Simulated, according to Vest’s own Prop Trading Terms: every account, Instant included, is simulated, and payouts are compensation from Vest’s corporate funds. Vest’s marketing calls it real capital; we go by the binding terms.
How do Vest Capital payouts work?+
Claim any time, as often as you like, with no cap. Your share (95% on Instant, 80% on the 1-Step cards and 2-Step, 70%-95% in the builder) moves to your Vest Primary Account within 24 hours, and you withdraw it as USDC to a crypto wallet. Withdrawals have a 1 USDC minimum and a $50,000 rolling 24-hour limit. No bank or fiat payout is documented.
How much does Vest Capital cost?+
One-time fees with no subscription or activation fee (Vest’s site, Nov 5, 2026): Instant $10 / $200 / $400 / $1,000 / $2,000 for $500 / $5K / $10K / $25K / $50K; 1-Step $75 / $150 / $375 / $745, or $40 / $80 / $210 / $415 with the 3% daily loss limit; 2-Step $15 / $35 / $90 / $175 (builder only); and a custom 1-Step from $20 to $2,170.
Does Vest Capital have a daily loss limit?+
Not on Instant accounts. On the 1-Step it is optional: none by default, or 3% on the landing-page cards for a lower price (3%, 4% or 5% in the builder). The 2-Step has a fixed 3% limit. It resets at 8:00 PM ET.
Does Vest Capital have a consistency rule?+
No. There is no consistency rule, no minimum number of trading days and no time limit on any Vest Capital plan.
How many Vest Capital accounts can I have?+
Up to 10 Live funded accounts at once (Instant accounts count) and up to 100 evaluation accounts, per Vest’s account-limit settings. Its terms word the 10-account cap as Instant accounts only, so check with Vest before you scale up.
Who runs Vest Capital, and is it legit?+
Vest Capital is the prop program of Vest Markets; the contracting party is Vest Exchange Inc., a Panama company, and disputes go to arbitration in Panama. Jane Street’s seed investment is confirmed by independent press, and Vest claims no license. In 2026 it reported three pricing incidents, including a full platform rollback on Apr 15, and a Philippine SEC advisory names Vest. We have not verified its payout record independently.
Is there a Vest Capital promo code?+
Not from PropChamps yet: we don’t have a Vest Capital code, and Vest’s site showed no discount on Nov 5, 2026. Vest’s custom-account builder has an “Add Code” field; codes other sites publish for Vest are their own affiliate codes, which we haven’t verified.
Can I get a refund from Vest Capital?+
Only within 3 days of purchase and only if you haven’t placed a trade. A failed evaluation is not refundable.
