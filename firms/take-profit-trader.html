PRO account

Take Profit Trader PRO account rules

A PRO account pays 80/20 and lets you withdraw from day one once your balance clears a buffer equal to the max drawdown. There are no minimum profit days, no consistency rule and no maximum withdrawal.

  • Balance needed before withdrawing: $26,500 / $52,000 / $77,500 / $103,000 / $154,500 (25K / 50K / 75K / 100K / 150K).
  • Drawdown: intraday trailing, stopping at the starting balance.
  • No bots or algos; you must trade at least once per calendar week.
  • News: flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI.
  • If the account closes, you can withdraw 50% of the buffer.
  • Wallet withdrawals of $250 or less carry a $50 fee.

PRO+ rules

PRO+ is a live account paying 90/10 with an end-of-day drawdown and no buffer. It starts at a $0 balance with the original drawdown, and $5,000 of your PRO profit stays frozen while PRO+ is active.

Drawdown

Is Take Profit Trader EOD or intraday drawdown?

Both, depending on the stage: the TEST uses an end-of-day trailing drawdown, PRO switches to intraday trailing (it stops at the starting balance), and PRO+ goes back to end-of-day with no buffer.

That switch from end-of-day to intraday is the biggest rule change between stages, so size down in your first PRO days. EOD vs intraday drawdown explained · drawdown calculator.

Limits

How many Take Profit Trader accounts can you have?

Up to 5 active funded accounts per person, PRO and PRO+ combined. Tests are unlimited, with at most 10 passed-test activations per 30 days.

Trade copiers are allowed on your own accounts with approved copiers only: Tradesyncer, TradeCopia, Affordable Indicators, Replikanto Compliance Edition, or a platform’s built-in copier. Account limits at every firm.

FAQ

Take Profit Trader FAQ

Is Take Profit Trader a monthly subscription?+
Yes. The TEST is billed monthly ($150 / $170 / $245 / $330 / $360 for 25K / 50K / 75K / 100K / 150K at our audit) and billing stops when you pass. Moving to PRO costs a one-time $130 activation fee, which promos often waive.
Does Take Profit Trader have a consistency rule?+
Only in the TEST: your best day must be below 50% of net profit. PRO and PRO+ have no consistency rule.
Does Take Profit Trader have a daily loss limit?+
No. There is no daily loss limit at any size or stage; the max drawdown is the only loss limit.
How fast are Take Profit Trader payouts?+
On PRO you can withdraw from day one once your balance clears the buffer. Account-to-wallet transfers on Tradovate are automated and take seconds; bank withdrawals go through a manual review of up to 12 business hours, then Plaid, PayPal or Wise.
How many Take Profit Trader accounts can I have?+
Up to 5 active funded accounts per person (PRO and PRO+ combined). Tests are unlimited, with at most 10 passed-test activations per 30 days. See account limits at every firm.
Can you trade news at Take Profit Trader?+
In the TEST, yes. On PRO and PRO+ you must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI. See our news trading rules.
How many days does it take to pass Take Profit Trader?+
At least 3 trading days for tests bought from Aug 17, 2026 (5 for older tests), and your best day must stay below 50% of net profit.
What platforms does Take Profit Trader support?+
Fourteen: TradingView, NinjaTrader, Tradovate, Bookmap, MultiCharts, MotiveWave, Trade Navigator, R|Trader, VolFix, ATAS, Investor R/T, Quantower, Finamark and Jigsaw Trader.
