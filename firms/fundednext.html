Pick Flex if

you want the highest split (95%) and no daily loss limit; payouts need 5 benchmark days, and the account ends after 5 rewards.

Pick Legacy if

Pick Rapid Daily if

you want daily rewards and no consistency rule at any stage, and can trade under a daily loss limit.

FundedNext Futures payout rules

Flex pays 95% after 5 benchmark days, Legacy 80% after 5 benchmark days, Rapid Pro 90% every 3 days and Rapid Daily 90% daily. Every funded account has a $250 minimum withdrawal, and rewards are requested at the end of the day.

Consistency

FundedNext Futures consistency rule

Flex and Legacy have a 40% consistency rule in the challenge and none once funded. Rapid Pro is the reverse: none in the challenge and 40% once funded. Rapid Daily has no consistency rule at any stage, and Direct uses a 20% perpetual rule.

Limits

How many FundedNext Futures accounts can you have?

Up to 5 active funded accounts per user and per household (same address or IP), with at most 10 issued per calendar month. Challenges can total up to $750K of combined allocation.

Copying your own trades between your own FundedNext accounts, and to your own accounts at other firms (same name, verified), is allowed; signal services and group trading are not. Account limits at every firm.

FAQ

FundedNext FAQ

Does FundedNext Futures use Tradovate?+
Yes. FundedNext Futures runs on NinjaTrader, Tradovate and TradingView.
Does FundedNext Futures allow news trading?+
Yes. News trading is allowed on every FundedNext Futures account type. See our news trading rules.
Does FundedNext Futures have a daily loss limit?+
Not on Flex, Legacy or Rapid Pro (Rapid Pro offers an optional DLL add-on at a lower price). Rapid Daily has one: $500 / $1,000 / $1,250. The new Direct account has a $1,000 DLL.
Does FundedNext Futures have an activation fee?+
No. Each challenge is a one-time fee with no activation fee.
Is FundedNext Futures the same as FundedNext’s CFD program?+
Same company, different product with its own rules. This review covers only the futures accounts. FundedNext’s published $380M+ payout total covers all of its programs, not futures alone.
How many FundedNext Futures accounts can I have?+
Up to 5 active funded accounts per user and per household (same address or IP), with at most 10 issued per calendar month. Challenges can total up to $750K of combined allocation. See account limits at every firm.
Does FundedNext Futures have instant funding?+
Yes, a new $50K Direct account: no challenge, a $2,100 buffer, a 20% perpetual consistency rule, $800-$1,200 per withdrawal and a 90% share. The offer price was $169.99 at our Oct 23, 2026 check.
Which FundedNext Futures account has no consistency rule?+
Rapid Daily: it has no consistency rule in the challenge or once funded. Flex and Legacy drop theirs once funded, and Rapid Pro only applies one once funded.
