Payouts

Payout rules at a glance

Select Daily pays daily from profit above a buffer. Growth and Select Flex pay after every 5 profitable or winning days. Lightning is marketed as 5-day payouts, with no fixed minimum days once you hit the profit goal and the consistency rule. Every funded account pays 90/10.

Limits

How many Tradeify accounts can you have?

Five sim-funded accounts per person and per household, in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning.

Holding an Elite Live account means no other accounts, for you or anyone in your household. Copying your own trades across your own accounts is allowed where the help center permits it, with no opposing positions; copying between people is prohibited.

FAQ

Tradeify FAQ

Is Tradeify a one-time fee?+
Yes. Since 03/31/26 every Tradeify plan (Growth, Select and Lightning) is a one-time payment; monthly subscriptions were removed.
Does Tradeify have an activation fee?+
No. There is no activation fee on any Tradeify plan.
Does Tradeify have a daily loss limit?+
Growth does ($600 / $1,250 / $2,500 / $3,750). Select has none in the evaluation; the Select Daily funded path adds one ($500 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,750), while Select Flex has none at any stage. Lightning has none on 25K and $1,250 / $2,500 / $3,000 on 50K-150K.
Does Tradeify pay out in crypto?+
Yes, crypto payouts are available on Growth accounts.
What platforms does Tradeify support?+
Tradovate, WealthCharts, Tradesea and Rithmic. The Tradesea and Rithmic data feeds were added on 03/31/26.
How many Tradeify accounts can I have?+
Five sim-funded accounts per person and per household, in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning.
Is Tradeify the same as TradeDay?+
No. They are different futures prop firms with similar names. This review covers Tradeify (tradeify.co).
