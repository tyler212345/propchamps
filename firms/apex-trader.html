Payout rules at a glance

Apex funded accounts (PAs) keep 100% of approved payouts. You can request after 5 qualifying trading days, with a $500 minimum, a 50% consistency rule and a cap per payout that rises over a maximum of 6 payouts per PA.

News & limits

Apex news rules and account limits

You can trade through news with your normal strategy, but news-only or straddle strategies and hedging are prohibited, and positions must be flat by market close. Apex allows up to 20 active PAs per household, with every PA type counted together.

Evaluations are unlimited. Apex’s own pages conflict on copy trading across your own PAs, so check with Apex before copying. Apex also brings back legacy accounts (6 sizes up to $300K) during sale periods.

FAQ

Apex Trader Funding FAQ

Is Apex Trader Funding a monthly subscription?+
No. Apex evaluations are a one-time fee with no rebill. Each evaluation is active for 30 days, and there are no resets.
Does Apex have an activation fee?+
On the Standard build, yes: you pay a PA activation fee after you pass. The No-Activation-Fee build costs more up front and skips it. Per Apex’s help center, coupon codes don’t apply to activation fees. We could not confirm the current fee amounts, so check them at checkout.
What is the difference between Apex Intraday Trail and EOD Trail?+
Both have the same targets ($1,500-$9,000), drawdown ($1,000-$4,000) and 1-day pass. Intraday Trail has no daily loss limit in the evaluation but trails your intraday peak; EOD Trail only updates the drawdown at the end of the day and has a $500-$2,000 daily loss limit. More in Apex drawdown explained.
Does Apex have a consistency rule?+
Only on funded PAs, not in the evaluations: no single trading day may be 50% or more of the profit since your last payout. Going over is not a breach; it delays your payout until more profitable days bring that day under 50% (per Apex’s help center, October 2026). See how consistency rules work.
Can you trade news on Apex?+
Yes, with your normal strategy. News-only or straddle strategies and hedging are prohibited, and you must be flat by market close. See our news trading rules.
How many Apex accounts can you have?+
Up to 20 active PAs per household, with every PA type counted together. Evaluations are unlimited.
What are Apex legacy accounts?+
Older-style accounts that Apex brings back during sale periods, in 6 sizes up to $300K. Per Apex’s help center, only the first 5 payouts on a legacy PA are capped.
Does Apex support TradingView?+
Yes, through Tradovate. Apex runs on Rithmic, Tradovate and WealthCharts.
