Tradeify Payout Rules (2026): Growth, Select Daily, Select Flex & Lightning

Tradeify has four funded paths, and each one pays out on a different trigger: profitable days, a daily buffer, winning days, or a profit goal. Here are the caps, minimums and rules for each path at every size, plus which one fits how you trade.

The short answer
  • Every Tradeify funded account pays 90/10. All plans are one-time payments with no activation fee.
  • Growth: payouts after 5 profitable days, 35% funded consistency, caps rising from $1,000–$2,500 on payout 1 to $1,000–$5,000 from payout 4†.
  • Select Daily: request daily from profit above a buffer ($1,100–$3,600), capped at $600–$2,500 per payout†, with no funded consistency rule.
  • Select Flex: every 5 winning days, up to 50% of profit capped at $1,250–$4,500†, with no daily loss limit and no funded consistency rule.
  • Lightning (instant funding): a payout profit goal plus 20% / 25% / 30% consistency, a $1,000 minimum†, and caps of $1,000–$3,000† (payouts 1–3).

Tradeify Select vs Growth

Growth has no consistency rule in the evaluation but a DLL; Select has no DLL in the evaluation but a 40% consistency rule and a 3-day minimum. Once funded it flips: Growth applies 35% consistency, while neither Select path has a consistency rule. On price, Select lists at $109 / $165 / $265 / $369 against Growth’s $99 / $145 / $255 / $369 (25K–150K).

Tradeify Select Daily vs Flex

Daily if you want cash every day and can live with a funded DLL ($500–$1,750) and caps of $600–$2,500. Flex if you want no DLL and bigger caps ($1,250–$4,500), and are fine waiting for 5 winning days and taking half your profit at a time.

Rules that apply to every Tradeify funded account

  • Hold time: at least 50% of your profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds (Funded Trader Agreement). The help center adds that more than 50% of your trades must also be held over 10 seconds†.
  • Activity: you must trade at least once per calendar week.
  • Accounts: up to 5 sim-funded accounts in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning, per individual and per household. Holding an Elite Live account means no other accounts, for you or anyone in your household. Other firms: how many prop firm accounts you can have.
  • Copy trading: allowed between your own accounts where Tradeify’s help center permits it†, with no opposing positions; copying between people is prohibited (Funded Trader Agreement 6.7(b)).

How to request a Tradeify payout

  1. Meet your path’s trigger. Growth: 5 profitable days and the balance threshold. Select Daily: profit above the buffer. Select Flex: 5 winning days. Lightning: the payout profit goal and consistency.
  2. Size the request between the minimum and the cap; on Select Flex no more than 50% of profit, and on Select Daily no more than 2× the profit since your last payout.
  3. Check the account rules: the 10-second rule and at least one trade per calendar week.
  4. Submit it from your Tradeify dashboard and confirm the amounts shown there, since our caps and minimums are help-center excerpts.

Why Tradeify payouts get held or denied

  • Consistency over the limit: 35% on funded Growth, or 20–30% on Lightning depending on the payout number.
  • Too few qualifying days: Growth and Flex days below the size threshold don’t count.
  • Balance or buffer not cleared on Growth or Select Daily.
  • Amount outside the limits: below the minimum ($1,000 on Lightning) or above the cap.
  • Short-hold trading: too much profit from trades held 10 seconds or less.
  • Account rules: more than 5 funded accounts in a household, opposing positions across your accounts, or copying another trader.

Common questions

Tradeify: frequently asked questions

What are Tradeify’s payout rules?+

Every funded path pays 90/10. Growth pays after 5 profitable days with 35% funded consistency; Select Daily pays daily from profit above a buffer; Select Flex pays after 5 winning days, up to 50% of profit; Lightning pays once you hit a profit goal with 20–30% consistency. Each path has its own caps and minimums.

What are the Tradeify Select payout rules?+

Select Daily: daily requests from profit above a $1,100 / $2,100 / $2,600 / $3,600 buffer, capped at $600 / $1,250 / $1,750 / $2,500, $250 minimum. Select Flex: after 5 winning days, up to 50% of profit capped at $1,250 / $2,500 / $3,500 / $4,500, $250 minimum. Neither has a funded consistency rule. Values run 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K; buffers and minimums are per Tradeify’s help center (Oct 2026), so confirm them in your dashboard.

What are the Tradeify Growth payout rules?+

You need 5 profitable days (over $100 / $150 / $200 / $250) and a minimum balance, with a 35% funded consistency rule. Payout 1 is capped at $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 and payout 4 onward at $1,000 / $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000 for 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K, per Tradeify’s help center (Oct 2026). Confirm them in your dashboard.

Tradeify Select vs Growth: which is better?+

Growth has no evaluation consistency rule but a DLL, and 35% consistency once funded. Select has a 40% evaluation rule and no evaluation DLL, and no consistency rule once funded, where you choose Daily or Flex payouts.

Tradeify Select Daily vs Flex: what is the difference?+

Select Daily lets you request every day from profit above a buffer, with a funded DLL and caps of $600–$2,500. Select Flex has no DLL and caps of $1,250–$4,500, but needs 5 winning days per payout and pays at most 50% of profit.

What are the Tradeify Lightning funded payout rules?+

Lightning has no evaluation. Each payout needs a profit goal ($1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000 for payout 1) and 20% / 25% / 30% consistency, with a $1,000 minimum and caps of $1,000 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000 for payouts 1–3, per Tradeify’s help center (Oct 2026), which also says there is no fixed minimum number of days. Confirm them in your dashboard.

Which Tradeify account has daily payouts?+

Select Daily. You pass the Select evaluation, choose the Daily path, and can then request every day from profit above the buffer.

What is the Tradeify max payout on a 50K account?+

Per request on 50K: Growth $1,500 rising to $3,000, Select Daily $1,250, Select Flex $2,500 (50% of profit), and Lightning $2,000 rising to $2,500. The Growth and Lightning caps are per Tradeify’s help center (Oct 2026); confirm them in your dashboard.

What is the Tradeify minimum payout?+

Growth $250 / $500 / $1,000 / $1,500 by size, Select Daily and Select Flex $250, and Lightning $1,000, per Tradeify’s help center. Confirm in your dashboard.

Does Tradeify pay out in crypto?+

Crypto payouts are available on Growth accounts.

How many Tradeify funded accounts can you have?+

Up to 5 sim-funded accounts in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning, per individual and per household.
