Key data points

Lucid runs four futures account paths: Pro (full ladder, 1-day pass, payouts on request with no fixed window), Flex (optional DLL, no funded consistency, scaling plan), Daily (configurable eval + daily payouts), and Direct (straight to funded, no eval). All pay a 90/10 split and use the LucidScale DLL model (60% of peak EOD balance above your initial trail) on funded accounts. Free activation on Pro, Flex and Daily. Platforms: Tradovate, NinjaTrader, TradingView, TradeSea, MotiveWave, Quantower. Code CHAMP applies at checkout.

Consistency

Lucid consistency rule

It depends on the path. Pro has no consistency rule in the evaluation and 40% once funded. Flex and Daily use 40% in the evaluation (50% with a paid add-on) and none once funded. Direct uses 20%.

Lucid checks the rule at payout time: your largest day divided by the account’s profit. On Pro it resets after each payout. In our arithmetic, a $800 best day on Pro means you need at least $2,000 of cycle profit before you can request ($800 ÷ 40%), and on Direct a $500 best day needs $2,500 ($500 ÷ 20%).

Pro accounts bought or reset before 11/28/2025 keep the older terms: 35% consistency and 100% of the first $10K. Because the rule is checked when you request a payout, going over it delays that payout until more profitable days bring the ratio down (our reading of Lucid’s rule).

Drawdown

Drawdown and the LucidScale DLL

Pro, Flex and Direct use an end-of-day (EOD) drawdown. Daily lets you choose EOD or intraday in the evaluation, but a funded Daily account is always intraday. The daily loss limit is optional on Pro, Flex and Daily and fixed on Direct.

News & hours

Lucid news trading and session hours

Pro, Flex and Direct let you trade through news (you carry the slippage risk). LucidDaily does not: trading red-folder USD news on a funded Daily account is a hard breach, so be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after.

All positions must be flat by 4:45 PM EST. Compare other firms in our news trading rules by firm.

Limits

How many Lucid accounts can you have?

Up to 10 active evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household, with every Lucid funded type counted together (for example 3 Direct + 2 Pro), and up to 5 live accounts.

Automated trading systems and trade copiers are permitted across your own accounts; hedging is not. If one household member is live, the others may not trade sim accounts.

FAQ

Lucid Trading FAQ

Is Lucid Trading legit?+
Lucid Trading publishes its full rulebook in a public help center, which is what we audit against: four account paths, a 90/10 split and a $500 minimum payout on every path, with approved payouts sent within 2 business days. We have not verified a Lucid payout total, so we don’t quote one. Track what we do verify on our payout tracker and audit log.
Which Lucid account is best?+
It depends on how you trade. Pro suits a fast pass with rising payout caps, Flex suits traders who want no consistency rule once funded, Daily suits daily payout requests if you avoid news, and Direct skips the evaluation for a higher one-time price. Which Lucid account is best compares them in depth.
Does Lucid Trading have a daily loss limit?+
Only if you choose one on Pro, Flex and Daily: the DLL is optional and set at purchase (Daily: $600 / $1,200 / $1,800 / $2,700; Pro: none on 25K, then $1,200 / $1,800 / $2,700). Direct has a fixed DLL of $1,200 / $2,100 / $3,000 on 50K-150K and none on 25K. Above the initial trail, funded accounts use the LucidScale DLL: 60% of your highest end-of-day profit.
Does Lucid allow news trading?+
Yes on Pro, Flex and Direct. LucidDaily is the exception: trading red-folder USD news on a funded Daily account is a hard breach, so be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after. See our news trading rules.
Is Lucid Black still available?+
No. Lucid Black is discontinued; only existing legacy accounts still run on it. New accounts are Pro, Flex, Daily or Direct.
How many Lucid accounts can I have?+
Up to 10 active evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household, with all Lucid funded types counted together. Trade copiers are allowed across your own accounts.
Does Lucid Trading have an activation fee?+
No. Activation is free on Pro, Flex and Daily. Direct is a single one-time payment that puts you straight into a funded account, so there is nothing to activate.
