Which Lucid Trading Account Is Best? Flex vs Pro vs Daily vs Direct

Lucid sells four accounts that look alike on the evaluation and behave very differently once you are funded. This page puts them side by side at every size and matches each one to the kind of trader it suits.

The short answer
  • LucidFlex if you want the lowest fixed entry price at most sizes ($90.20 for 50K with CHAMP; LucidDaily is priced at checkout) and no consistency rule once funded. The cost: 5 profit days per payout and payouts of at most 50% of profit, capped at $1,000–$3,000.
  • LucidPro if you want to pass in one day (no evaluation consistency rule) and bigger caps after payout 1 ($1,500–$3,500). Once funded you face a 40% rule and a buffer.
  • LucidDaily if you want to request a payout every day with no per-payout cap. The cost: an intraday drawdown when funded, and red-folder news is a hard breach.
  • LucidDirect if you want to skip the evaluation. It costs more up front than a Pro or Flex evaluation ($230.30–$585.20 with CHAMP) and pairs a 20% consistency rule with a profit goal before every payout.
  • Flex is cheaper than Pro with CHAMP at 25K, 50K, 100K; at 150K Pro is slightly cheaper.

The four Lucid accounts in one minute

Low entry price

LucidFlex

$90.20 for 50K with CHAMP. Same target and max loss as Pro, no funded consistency rule, no buffer.

Best for: traders who have big days, and anyone on a tight budget.

Watch out: 5 profit days per payout, and each payout is at most 50% of profit ($1,000–$3,000 cap).

Fastest pass

LucidPro

No consistency rule in the evaluation, so you can pass in one day. Caps step up after payout 1.

Best for: traders who want to pass quickly and withdraw larger amounts per payout.

Watch out: 40% funded consistency, plus a buffer of the initial max loss + $100 before any payout.

Daily payouts

LucidDaily

Request every day once funded, with no per-payout cap above the buffer. The evaluation is configurable.

Best for: traders who want to cash out often and don’t trade the news.

Watch out: Intraday drawdown when funded; red-folder news is a hard breach.

No evaluation

LucidDirect

Straight to funded for a one-time $230.30–$585.20 with CHAMP.

Best for: traders who would rather pay more than pass an evaluation.

Watch out: 20% consistency and a profit goal before every payout ($1,500–$9,000 for the first).

Lucid Flex vs Pro: the difference that matters

On the evaluation they are close: the same $3,000 target and $2,000 end-of-day max loss on 50K. The differences are the evaluation consistency rule (none on Pro, 40% on Flex) and everything after you pass.

At 50K, LucidPro costs $115.40 with CHAMP. Every payout needs the $500 cycle goal, a balance above $52,100 and a best day under 40% of profit. In return you can take up to $2,000 on payout 1 and $2,500 after.

LucidFlex costs $90.20. There is no buffer and no funded consistency rule, but each payout waits for 5 profit days of $150+ and is limited to 50% of profit, up to $2,000. To take the full $2,000 you need $4,000 of profit in the cycle (our arithmetic).

In short: Pro suits traders whose days are even and who want bigger withdrawals; Flex suits traders with uneven days who are happy to withdraw half at a time. The full payout mechanics are in our Lucid Trading payout rules.

LucidDaily vs LucidPro

Both use the same buffer ($52,100 on 50K). After that they split. LucidDaily lets you request every day with no per-payout cap and no funded consistency rule. Its funded drawdown is intraday, and a red-folder news trade ends the account. LucidPro keeps an end-of-day drawdown and allows news, but caps each payout and applies a 40% rule. LucidDaily’s evaluation has a 40% rule (50% add-on); Pro’s has none.

LucidFlex vs LucidDirect

LucidDirect trades money for time: $360.50 at 50K versus $90.20 for a Flex evaluation, and no evaluation to pass. Once funded it is the stricter account: a $3,000 profit goal before payout 1, a 20% consistency rule, and a DLL on 50K and up. LucidFlex makes you pass first, then has no funded consistency rule and only needs 5 profit days per payout. Both cap payouts at $2,000 on 50K at the start; Direct rises to $2,500 for payouts 4–5.

Rules that apply to every Lucid account

  • Split and minimum: 90/10 split, $500 minimum request, no fixed payout window.
  • Account limits: up to 10 evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household, all Lucid types combined (10 in total).
  • Copy trading: trade copiers and automated systems are permitted; hedging is not.
  • Size: 2 / 4 / 6 / 10 minis (20 / 40 / 60 / 100 micros) by account size, on every path.
  • Session: all positions flat by 4:45 PM EST.
  • Lucid Black is discontinued (legacy accounts only).

Common questions

Lucid Trading: frequently asked questions

Which Lucid Trading account is best?+

It depends on how you trade. LucidFlex has the lowest fixed price at most sizes and no funded consistency rule. LucidPro has a 1-day pass and bigger caps after payout 1. LucidDaily pays daily with no per-payout cap but uses an intraday funded drawdown and bans red-folder news. LucidDirect skips the evaluation for a higher one-time price and a 20% consistency rule.

What is the difference between Lucid Flex and Pro?+

Pro has no evaluation consistency rule (you can pass in a day) but a 40% funded rule, a buffer and caps of $1,000–$3,000 on payout 1. Flex has a 40% evaluation rule, no funded rule and no buffer, but each payout needs 5 profit days and is limited to 50% of profit, capped at $1,000–$3,000.

Should I buy a 50K Lucid Flex or a 50K Pro?+

At 50K, Flex costs $90.20 with CHAMP and Pro costs $115.40. Flex pays up to 50% of profit per payout (max $2,000) after 5 profit days of $150+. Pro pays up to $2,000 then $2,500 but needs a balance above $52,100 and a best day under 40% of profit.

LucidDaily vs LucidPro: which is better?+

LucidDaily if you want to request every day with no per-payout cap and you don’t trade news: its funded drawdown is intraday and red-folder news is a hard breach. LucidPro if you want an end-of-day drawdown, news trading and a 1-day pass, and can live with a 40% rule and per-payout caps.

LucidFlex vs LucidDirect: which should I choose?+

LucidDirect skips the evaluation but costs more ($360.50 at 50K vs $90.20) and is stricter once funded: a profit goal before every payout and a 20% consistency rule. LucidFlex needs an evaluation, then has no funded consistency rule.

What is the best Lucid Trading account for beginners?+

No account is best for everyone. For a first account, cost and simple rules usually matter most: LucidFlex is cheaper than Pro with CHAMP at most sizes and has no funded consistency rule. If passing in a single day matters more to you, LucidPro has no evaluation consistency rule.

Does Lucid Trading have an activation fee?+

No activation fee on Pro, Flex or Daily. LucidDirect is a single one-time payment that includes activation.

How many Lucid Trading accounts can you have?+

Up to 10 evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household, with every Lucid type counted together (10 in total).

Can you trade news on Lucid Trading?+

Yes on Pro, Flex and Direct. On a funded LucidDaily account, trading red-folder USD news is a hard breach; you must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after. See news trading rules for every firm.

Is Lucid Black still available?+

No. Lucid Black is discontinued; only legacy accounts remain. The current paths are Pro, Flex, Daily and Direct.
