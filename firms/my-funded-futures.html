Key data points

My Funded Futures runs four products as of August 2026: Rapid (daily payouts, 90/10, intraday-trailing once funded), Rapid EOD (25K/50K, daily payouts but end-of-day drawdown in funded), Pro (bi-weekly payouts, 80/20, up to $100K in sim payouts per user), and Builder (25K-150K eval, 48-hour payouts, and a five-payout ladder to a real Live account at Blue Row Capital). All plans are one-time purchases (no monthly renewals, effective Aug 2026). Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro have no daily loss limit (Builder has a soft daily pause), and no plan has an activation fee. Payouts

My Funded Futures payout rules

Rapid and Rapid EOD pay daily at 90/10 with a $500 minimum once you clear the buffer. Pro pays every 14 calendar days at 80/20 with a $1,000 minimum. Builder pays at 80/20 from 48 hours after your first trade, with a cap per cycle and a route to Live after 5 payouts.

How MFF moves you to a live account

  • Rapid: a $10,000 net-profit session moves you to Live automatically.
  • Pro: 3 consecutive payouts or $20K of profit triggers a Live review.
  • Builder: 5 approved sim payouts unlock a real Live account at Blue Row Capital: daily payouts, 80/20, end-of-day drawdown, a live DLL ($1,000 on 50K) and no consistency rule.
Drawdown

MFF drawdown: Rapid switches to intraday once funded

Rapid uses an end-of-day drawdown in the evaluation, then an intraday trailing drawdown once funded: the funded account starts at $0 and the trail locks at +$100. Rapid EOD keeps an end-of-day drawdown when funded. Pro and Builder trail end-of-day.

News

MFF Tier-1 news rule

Tier-1 news (FOMC, NFP and CPI, plus EIA and agricultural reports for those markets) is prohibited on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after. It is allowed in every evaluation and on Builder.

Limits

How many MFF accounts can you have?

Up to 10 active evaluations and 5 sim-funded accounts if all of them are 25K or 50K, or 3 if any is 100K or 150K. Builder allows 1 sim-funded account per user (2 on 25K), and Rapid EOD 3.

Copy trading is allowed across all of your own accounts (Tradesyncer, Tradovate group trading or an external copier).

FAQ

My Funded Futures FAQ

Is My Funded Futures a monthly fee?+
No. Since August 2026 every MFF plan is a one-time purchase with no monthly renewal.
Does My Funded Futures have a daily loss limit?+
Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro have none. Builder has a soft daily pause of $600 / $1,000 / $1,750 / $2,500, and Builder’s real Live account has a DLL.
Does My Funded Futures charge an activation fee?+
No. Activation is $0 on every plan.
Does MFF have a consistency rule?+
Only in the evaluation on Rapid (50%), Rapid EOD (30%) and Pro (50%), with none once funded. Builder is the reverse: no evaluation consistency, and 50% at the payout stage.
Does MFF move you to a live account?+
Yes, three ways: a $10,000 net-profit session on Rapid moves you Live automatically; Pro gets a Live review after 3 consecutive payouts or $20K of profit; Builder gives a real Live account at Blue Row Capital after 5 approved sim payouts.
How many MFF accounts can I have?+
Up to 10 active evaluations and 5 sim-funded accounts if all are 25K/50K (3 if any is 100K/150K). Builder allows 1 sim-funded account per user (2 on 25K), and Rapid EOD 3. Copy trading across your own accounts is allowed.
How fast are My Funded Futures payouts?+
Most payouts are approved instantly; manual reviews take 6-12 business hours. Rapid can pay daily, with the first payout 24 hours after your first funded trade.
What happened to MFF Flex?+
MFF retired the Flex plan. The current lineup is Rapid, Rapid EOD, Pro and Builder.
