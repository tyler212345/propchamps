Alpha Futures Payout Rules (2026): Zero, Standard, Advanced & Direct

All four Alpha plans pay 90% from the first withdrawal, up to four times a month. The minimum, the cap and what you must do before each request depend on the plan. Here is each one, by account size.

The short answer
  • Every plan pays 90% from the first payout (no tiers), allows up to 4 withdrawals a month, and has no assigned payout dates.
  • Zero, Standard and Advanced: withdraw up to 50% of your profit per request, after 5 winning days of $200 or more since your last request.
  • Direct: no winning-day rule. Hit the withdrawal profit target ($1,500–$9,000 first, then $1,000–$6,000) and stay under 20% consistency.
  • Minimums: $200 Zero · $500 Standard · $1,000 Advanced · $500 Direct. Caps per request: $1,000–$2,500 Zero · $3,000–$5,000 Standard · $15,000 Advanced · $1,000–$3,000 Direct.
  • Qualified consistency: 40% on Zero and Standard, none on Advanced, 20% on Direct. Going over pauses payouts; it does not breach the account.

How the 5-winning-day counter and the 50% limit work

On Zero, Standard and Advanced, two things decide each request: the winning-day counter and the 50%-of-profit limit. A “winning day” only counts if it makes $200 or more.

Worked example · first withdrawal on a 50K Zero
DayP&LCounts?
1+$350Yes (1)
2−$120No
3+$210Yes (2)
4+$180No, under $200
5+$500Yes (3)
6+$260Yes (4)
7+$220Yes (5)

Five winning days of $200+ by day 7, and net profit is $1,600. The largest day ($500) is 31% of that, under the 40% qualified rule.

50% of $1,600 is $800: under the 50K cap of $1,500 and over the $200 minimum. Alpha takes $800 off the account and you receive 90%, $720. The max loss limit does not reset when you withdraw, so the $800 comes out of your cushion above the floor. Then the counter starts again from zero. (Our arithmetic from Alpha’s rules.)

Alpha’s consistency rule on qualified accounts

On a qualified account, your largest day must be below 40% (20% on Direct) of your net profit since the last request. The count resets every time you request. If you go over, Alpha pauses payouts until more profit brings the ratio back down. It does not breach the account.

The Zero vs Advanced question comes down to rules versus price: Advanced removes the DLL and the qualified consistency rule, and lets you trade news, for $70 more a month at 50K ($209 vs $139 list).

News, resets and other rules that affect payouts

  • News: no restrictions on any evaluation or on Advanced qualified accounts. On Zero, Standard and Direct qualified accounts you may hold positions through news, but no orders may execute within 2 minutes either side of a red-folder event. A first violation is a warning; later ones breach the account. See news trading rules.
  • Qualified resets (Zero $399–$799, Standard $599–$999) can be used twice per account, only before your first payout request. Advanced has no qualified reset.
  • Monthly billing stops when you pass. Qualified accounts have no monthly fee, and activation is $0 on every plan.
  • Max qualified accounts: Zero 5 · Standard 5 (3 if bought before Jul 27, 2026) · Direct 5 · Advanced 3. One individual per household. Other firms: how many prop firm accounts you can have.
  • Copy trading across accounts in your own name is allowed. Automated, group and reverse trading are not.

How to request an Alpha Futures payout

  1. Check the trigger. Zero, Standard, Advanced: 5 winning days of $200+ since your last request. Direct: the withdrawal profit target since your last request.
  2. Check consistency. Largest day below 40% (Direct 20%) of net profit since the last request. Advanced qualified accounts have no rule.
  3. Size the request. At least the plan minimum and no more than the cap; on Zero, Standard and Advanced, no more than 50% of profit.
  4. Submit it. You can request up to 4 times a month, on any day. You receive 90% of the amount, paid by ACH, wire/SWIFT, Wise and Rise.

Why Alpha Futures payouts get held or denied

  • Winning days under $200 don’t count toward the five. A +$180 day is a winning day, but not a qualifying one.
  • Largest day too big: 40% or more (20% on Direct) of net profit since your last request pauses payouts.
  • Amount outside the limits: under the minimum, over the cap, or over 50% of profit.
  • Fifth request in a month: the limit is 4 withdrawals a month.
  • News orders within 2 minutes of a red-folder event on a qualified Zero, Standard or Direct account: a warning the first time, a breach after that.
  • Account rules: more than the account limit, a second person in the household, or group or reverse trading.

Common questions

Alpha Futures: frequently asked questions

What are Alpha Futures’ payout rules?+

All plans pay 90% from the first payout, up to 4 withdrawals a month. Zero, Standard and Advanced let you withdraw up to 50% of profit after 5 winning days of $200+ since your last request. Direct uses a withdrawal profit target plus a 20% consistency rule instead. Minimums run from $200 (Zero) to $1,000 (Advanced).

What are the Alpha Futures Zero plan payout rules?+

After 5 winning days of $200+ you can withdraw up to 50% of profit, capped at $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,500 for 25K / 50K / 100K, with a $200 minimum. Qualified Zero accounts have a 40% consistency rule and a daily loss guard of $500 / $1,000 / $2,000.

What are the Alpha Futures Standard plan payout rules?+

The same 5-winning-day and 50%-of-profit rules, with caps of $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000 for 50K / 100K / 150K and a $500 minimum. Standard has 40% consistency once qualified (50% in the evaluation) and a qualified DLL of $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000.

What are the Alpha Futures Advanced plan payout rules?+

After 5 winning days of $200+ you can withdraw up to 50% of profit, up to $15,000 per request at every size, with a $1,000 minimum. Qualified Advanced accounts have no consistency rule, no daily loss limit and no news restrictions.

What is the Alpha Futures max payout?+

Per request: Zero $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,500, Standard $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000, Advanced $15,000 at every size, Direct $1,000 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000. Zero, Standard and Advanced are also limited to 50% of profit per request.

What is the Alpha Futures minimum payout?+

$200 on Zero, $500 on Standard, $1,000 on Advanced and $500 on Direct.

How often can you withdraw from Alpha Futures?+

Up to 4 times a month, with no assigned payout dates. Each request on Zero, Standard or Advanced needs 5 new winning days of $200+; on Direct it needs a new withdrawal profit target.

Does the drawdown reset after an Alpha Futures payout?+

No. Alpha’s max loss limit does not reset when you withdraw, so the amount you take out comes straight off your cushion above the floor.

Does Alpha Futures have a consistency rule?+

Yes, on most plans. Qualified accounts: 40% on Zero and Standard, 20% on Direct, none on Advanced. Evaluations: 50% on Standard, 40% on Advanced, none on Zero. Going over pauses payouts but does not breach the account.

Which Alpha Futures plan is best?+

Zero for the lowest entry price ($89 for 25K) and a 1-day pass, Standard for bigger caps, Advanced for the fewest rules (no DLL, no qualified consistency, no news restrictions, $15,000 per request), and Direct to skip the evaluation entirely.

What happened to Alpha Futures Premium?+

Premium was a short-lived plan that briefly replaced Standard and was then dropped. Standard is back on Alpha’s site; the current plans are Zero, Standard, Advanced and Direct.

How many Alpha Futures accounts can you have?+

Qualified accounts: Zero 5, Standard 5 (3 if bought before Jul 27, 2026), Direct 5 and Advanced 3. Holding any active Advanced qualified account caps you at 3. One individual per household.
