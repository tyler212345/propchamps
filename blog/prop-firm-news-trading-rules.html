Can You Trade News at a Prop Firm? Rules by Firm

Usually yes in the evaluation, and often with limits once you're funded. Apex, FundedNext and Alpha's Advanced plan allow news trading; TPT, MFF and Top One restrict funded accounts around high-impact releases; LucidDaily bans it outright.

The short version
  • Most futures prop firms let you trade news in the evaluation. The restrictions, when they exist, land on the funded account.
  • Apex allows trading through news as part of a normal strategy but bans news-only (straddle) strategies and hedging. FundedNext allows news on its accounts. Alpha's Advanced plan has no news restrictions at any stage.
  • Take Profit Trader (PRO, PRO+), My Funded Futures (funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro) and Top One (funded Elite Access and Elite Daily) restrict trading around high-impact releases.
  • Lucid allows news on Pro, Flex and Direct; on LucidDaily red-folder news is a hard breach.
  • The windows are short but strict: 1 minute either side at TPT and LucidDaily, 2 minutes at MFF, Alpha and Top One.

Can you hold a position through the FOMC decision or the NFP print on a funded account? It depends entirely on the firm — and news trading is one area where the rules differ more than almost anywhere else. Some firms let you trade every release freely; others will close your account if you're holding through a red-folder event, even when you're profitable and well inside your drawdown. Here's every firm's actual news-trading rule, checked against their help centers in October 2026.

The good news for futures traders: prop firms in this space are far more permissive on news than the FX/CFD world. The catch is that the restriction, when there is one, almost always lands on the funded account, not the evaluation — so you can trade news freely to pass, then get caught out once you're actually being paid.

What counts as "news trading"

News-trading rules are about scheduled, high-impact economic releases — the "Tier-1" or "red-folder" events on the economic calendar: FOMC rate decisions, Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP), CPI inflation prints, and GDP. Some firms also flag instrument-specific events: My Funded Futures, for example, adds EIA and agricultural reports for the markets they move.

The rules are almost never about general intraday volatility — they target these specific, calendar-scheduled moments when price can gap violently in a second. If a firm restricts news, it means holding or opening a position in a defined window around one of these releases.

Why prop firms restrict news trading

A high-impact release can gap the market straight through your stop, turning a controlled loss into a much larger one the firm has to absorb. Restrictions protect the firm's risk — and shut down a specific gamble where a trader opens both a long and a short right before a release, closes the loser, and lets the winner run. Because the risk is concrete, breaking a news rule can cost the account: on LucidDaily it's a hard breach, while Alpha gives one warning before it breaches.

News trading rules by firm

Can you trade news on Apex? (eval and PA)

Yes, in both the evaluation and the funded Performance Account, as long as it's part of your normal strategy. Apex prohibits dedicated news-only or straddle strategies (opening both sides right before a release to game the move) and hedging, and positions must be flat by market close. Apex's site blocked our October re-check, so this is from our Oct 5, 2026 audit.

Does Lucid allow news trading?

On Pro, Flex and Direct, yes: Lucid permits trading around news, and you carry the slippage risk. LucidDaily is the exception. Trading red-folder USD news on a funded Daily account is a hard breach, so you must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after the release. On every Lucid plan, positions must be flat by 4:45 PM EST.

Can you trade news on My Funded Futures?

In the evaluation, yes, and on Builder at every stage. Funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts may not trade Tier-1 news (FOMC, NFP and CPI, plus EIA and agricultural reports for those markets) from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after the release.

Take Profit Trader news rule

The TEST is unrestricted. On the PRO (sim-funded) and PRO+ (live) accounts you must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI.

Can you trade news on Tradeify?

The rules we track list no news restriction for Tradeify, but its help center blocked our October 2026 check, so we can't confirm it's unchanged; check before you trade a release. One rule we did confirm in Tradeify's Funded Trader Agreement matters for quick news scalps: at least 50% of your account profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds.

Top One Futures news rule

Funded Elite Access and Elite Daily accounts may not trade from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact news. Instant Sim Funded and Ignite have no news rule, and the rule doesn't apply to evaluations.

Can you trade news on Alpha Futures?

Every Alpha evaluation is unrestricted, and so is the Advanced qualified account. On Zero, Standard and Direct qualified accounts you can hold a position through a red-folder event, but no order may execute within 2 minutes either side of it. A first violation gets a warning; later ones breach the account.

Does FundedNext allow news trading?

Yes. FundedNext Futures allows news trading on Flex, Legacy and Rapid, in the challenge and once funded.

How to stay compliant

  • Know your firm's exact restricted-event list and the window: 1 minute either side at Take Profit Trader and LucidDaily, 2 minutes at My Funded Futures, Alpha and Top One.
  • On a restricted funded account, be flat before FOMC, NFP, and CPI. Set an alarm for the release and close out early rather than cutting it fine.
  • Don't hedge both sides of an event on firms that allow directional news trading (like Apex) — that, and dedicated news-only strategies, are what they still ban.
  • Check evaluation vs. funded. On most firms the rule only bites once you're funded, so passing with a news trade doesn't mean you're clear afterward.
  • Re-verify before big releases. Firms adjust their restricted-event lists, and instrument-specific events (oil inventories, bond auctions) are easy to miss.
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you trade news on a prop firm account?+

On most futures prop firms, yes, at least in the evaluation. Apex (with a normal strategy), FundedNext and Alpha's Advanced plan allow it once funded too. The common restriction is on funded accounts around high-impact events like FOMC, NFP and CPI, at Take Profit Trader, My Funded Futures, Top One and on Alpha's Zero, Standard and Direct plans.

Which prop firms allow news trading with no restrictions?+

FundedNext allows news on Flex, Legacy and Rapid, and Alpha's Advanced plan has no news restrictions at any stage. Apex allows news trading as part of a normal strategy (no straddles or hedging). Lucid's Pro, Flex and Direct and Top One's Instant Sim Funded and Ignite also have no news rule.

What happens if you trade during restricted news?+

It depends on the firm. On a funded LucidDaily account it's a hard breach. Alpha gives a warning for the first violation and breaches the account after that. At other firms, read the wording in the rules before you test it, because it can cost the account even when you're profitable and inside your drawdown.

Do prop firms restrict news in the evaluation or the funded phase?+

Usually the funded phase. Take Profit Trader, Top One, My Funded Futures and Alpha all let you trade news freely in the evaluation, then restrict it on some or all funded accounts. Always confirm which phase the rule applies to.

What is the flat-before-news rule?+

It's a requirement to have no open positions in a window around a scheduled release: 1 minute before to 1 minute after at Take Profit Trader and LucidDaily, and 2 minutes either side at My Funded Futures and Top One. Alpha's version is slightly different: you may hold through the event, but no order may execute in the 2-minute window.

Which futures prop firm is best for news trading?+

If news trading is central to your strategy, FundedNext allows it on all its account types, Alpha Futures' Advanced plan has no news restrictions at any stage, and Apex allows it as part of a normal strategy. Lucid's Pro, Flex and Direct work too. Weigh news freedom alongside drawdown, consistency and payout rules on our comparison page.

Can you trade news on an instant-funding account?+

On some. Top One's Instant Sim Funded and Ignite have no news rule and Lucid Direct permits trading around news, while Alpha Direct follows Alpha's 2-minute no-execution window around red-folder events. Compare the instant plans side by side in our best instant funding futures prop firms list.
