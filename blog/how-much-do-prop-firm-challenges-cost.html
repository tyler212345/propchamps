How Much Does a Prop Firm Challenge Cost?

A 50K futures evaluation lists anywhere from $39 to $1,190 before discount codes, and the fee is only part of the bill: activation fees, resets and monthly rebills decide what you really pay. Here is every firm's 50K cost, fee by fee.

The sticker price on a prop firm challenge is rarely what you actually pay. Between evaluation fees, activation fees, resets, and monthly rebills, the true cost of getting funded varies a lot — and the cheapest headline isn't always the cheapest path. Here's how futures prop firm pricing actually breaks down in 2026.

Most traders look at one number — the challenge fee — and stop there. But that fee is only the entry ticket. What you pay to actually reach a payout depends on how the firm structures activation, resets, and rebills, and on whether you pass on the first try. Two firms with the same headline price can cost wildly different amounts by the time you're funded.

The four costs of getting funded

  1. Evaluation / challenge fee — what you pay to start. This is the number in the ad.
  2. Activation fee — a separate charge a few firms apply when you pass and want to activate the funded account ($130 at Take Profit Trader, $139–$359 at Top One Elite Access).
  3. Reset fee — if you fail but don't want to rebuy the whole challenge, a reset restarts your evaluation for a smaller fee.
  4. Monthly rebill — on subscription firms, you pay again every month until you pass. Take three months and you've paid three times.

One-time vs. monthly pricing

This is the single biggest factor in true cost. One-time firms (Tradeify, Apex, and one-time plans elsewhere) charge once — if you take two months to pass, you still paid once. Subscription firms (Alpha, Top One's Elite Daily, Take Profit Trader) charge monthly until you pass, so a slow pass multiplies the cost. At all three the subscription stops once you pass.

The rule of thumb: if you're confident you'll pass quickly, a low monthly price can be the cheapest entry. If you expect it to take a while — or you've failed before — a one-time plan caps your downside. Check our active deals for the current code on each.

Activation fees by firm

An activation fee is charged after you pass, to switch on the funded account. Most firms we track have dropped it:

  • None: Tradeify, My Funded Futures, FundedNext, Lucid (on its evaluations) and Alpha Futures. Alpha's Advanced plan has had no activation fee for accounts bought after Jul 8, 2026.
  • Top One Futures: Elite Access charges $139 / $189 / $259 / $359 (25K–150K) after its $39 evaluation; the Elite Access Intraday variant is $69 / $94 / $129 / $179. Elite Daily, Instant Sim Funded and Ignite have none.
  • Take Profit Trader: a one-time $130 for the PRO account, often waived by promotion.
  • Apex Trader Funding: Standard builds charge one after you pass; No-Activation builds cost more up front and skip it. Per Apex's help center, coupon codes don't apply to activation fees.

That's why the cheapest evaluation isn't always the cheapest path: a $39 Top One Elite Access evaluation becomes $228 on a 50K once you activate it.

Hidden costs to watch

  • Activation fees — the classic surprise. You pass, then discover a charge to turn on the funded account: $130 at Take Profit Trader (often waived), $139–$359 at Top One Elite Access.
  • Reset fees vs. rebuys — failing and resetting is cheaper than rebuying, but not free; know the reset price before you start.
  • Data feed fees — most firms include the data feed, but confirm it, especially on live-market accounts.
  • Rebills on subscriptions — the quiet cost multiplier. A $170-a-month 50K at Take Profit Trader is $510 at list if it takes you three months.
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a prop firm challenge cost?+

A 50K futures evaluation lists from $39 (Top One Elite Access, plus a $189 activation fee) to $1,190 (Apex EOD Trail No-Activation), with most one-time plans between about $75 and $600 and monthly plans at $129–$218 a month. Discount codes cut the evaluation price, often heavily, so the list price is rarely what you pay.

Are one-time or monthly prop firms cheaper?+

It depends how fast you pass. One-time firms charge once regardless of how long you take, so they're cheaper for a slow pass. Monthly firms can be cheaper if you pass in the first billing cycle but get expensive if it takes several months.

What is an activation fee?+

An activation fee is a separate charge some firms apply after you pass the evaluation, to activate the funded account. Among the firms we track only Top One Elite Access ($139–$359), Take Profit Trader ($130, often waived) and Apex's Standard builds charge one, so factor it into your total cost.

Do prop firm discount codes really work?+

Yes, on the evaluation fee. Code CHAMP is listed at 90% off at Apex, 60% at Top One, up to 50% at My Funded Futures and 40% at Alpha, applied at checkout; at Take Profit Trader it applies at checkout, and TPT’s own public code NOFEE40 took 40% off at our Oct 27, 2026 check. At Apex, coupon codes don't cover resets or activation fees; at other firms, check whether yours does before you count on it, and confirm the final price before you pay.

How much does Apex cost?+

Apex sells four builds per size, all one-time with no rebill and no resets. At list: Intraday Trail Standard $167 / $249 / $790 / $1,190 (25K–150K); Intraday No-Activation $490 / $590 / $1,890 (50K–150K); EOD Trail Standard $490 / $590 / $1,190 / $2,190; EOD No-Activation $1,090 / $1,190 / $1,590 / $2,490. Standard builds add an activation fee after you pass; No-Activation builds skip it. Code CHAMP takes 90% off the evaluation at checkout.
