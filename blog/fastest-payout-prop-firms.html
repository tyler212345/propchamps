If you pass a futures prop firm's evaluation, the next question is the one that actually matters: how fast can you get your money out? We track payout speed across every major firm — both how often you can request a withdrawal and how long the cash takes to land once you do. Here's what the data shows in 2026.

The short version: Apex Trader Funding and My Funded Futures process payouts fastest — a median of about four hours at Apex, often near-instant at MFF — with Top One Futures close behind. But "fastest" depends on which half of the process you mean — request frequency and processing speed are two different things, and firms love to blur them.

Payout speed actually has two parts

When a firm advertises "fast payouts," it's usually talking about one of two things — and they're rarely the same:

  1. Eligibility and frequency — how soon after getting funded, and how often, you're allowed to hit the withdraw button. This can be daily, every five trading days, or gated behind a benchmark cycle.
  2. Processing time — how long the money takes to reach your account once the request is in. This is the number in the table above.

A firm can be excellent at one and mediocre at the other. Take Profit Trader lets PRO traders request daily, but median processing runs about a day. Apex gates requests to every five trading days, yet pays in roughly four hours. So if your goal is "money in my account as fast as possible after I click request," processing time wins. If it's "how frequently can I pull profit out," frequency wins. Decide which you actually care about before you compare.

The fastest firms for actually getting paid

Apex Trader Funding — ~4 hours. Among the fastest processing we track, backed by the deepest history ($800M+ paid, largest single payout we estimate at around $58K). You keep 100% of every approved payout — there's no split. The trade-off: payouts open every five trading days, and there's a 50% consistency rule on the funded account.

My Funded Futures — near-instant. The Rapid plan pays daily, has no daily loss limit, and splits 90/10. That combination — daily eligibility plus same-day-ish processing — makes it one of the quickest paths from profit to cash.

Top One Futures — under 12 hours. All four of Top One's paths pay a flat 90% split, and the Elite Daily plan allows daily requests. Consistent sub-12-hour processing keeps it near the top.

FundedNext — ~24 hours. It brings one of the biggest reported track records of any firm we cover ($380M+ across its programs) and the highest split on this list after Apex — 95% on the Flex account. On Flex, requests open after a five-day benchmark cycle, so it's slightly slower to your first withdrawal, but the terms afterward are strong.

What actually delays your payout

It's almost never the bank transfer. The usual culprits are rules baked into the account:

  • Minimum trading days. Many firms want five or more active trading days before your first request — FundedNext (a 5-day benchmark) and the 5-trading-day cycles at Apex and Tradeify all apply here.
  • Consistency rules. A cap on how much of your total profit can come from one day (often 30–50%). Hit your whole target in a single big session and you may have to keep trading to "balance" the account before you can withdraw.
  • First-payout thresholds and buffers. Some accounts need a profit buffer above the starting balance before the trailing drawdown locks, or enforce a minimum payout amount ($250–$1,000+).
  • Requesting on the wrong day. Firms that pay on a five-day cycle reset the clock — miss the window and you wait a full cycle for the next one.

None of these show up in a "we pay in 4 hours" headline, but they're what actually determine when you see money.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Which prop firm pays out the fastest?+

Based on our tracked estimates, Apex Trader Funding (roughly four hours from request to paid) and My Funded Futures (often near-instant) have the fastest median processing times, followed by Top One Futures (under 12 hours). If you care about requesting frequently rather than raw speed, the daily-payout plans at My Funded Futures, Take Profit Trader, and Top One are worth a look.

How long do prop firm payouts usually take?+

Most major futures firms process payouts within one to two business days once requested, and the fastest — Apex, My Funded Futures, Top One — often complete within hours. The bigger variable is how soon you're eligible to request in the first place, which depends on minimum trading days and each firm's payout cycle.

Can you get a prop firm payout every day?+

On some plans, yes. My Funded Futures Rapid, Take Profit Trader's PRO account, and Top One's Elite Daily all allow daily payout requests. Others gate requests to a five-trading-day cycle (Apex, Tradeify Growth) or a benchmark period (FundedNext Flex and Legacy), so you can't withdraw every day even if you're profitable.

Do futures prop firms actually pay?+

The major ones have verifiable, published track records — FundedNext reports $380M+, Apex $800M+, My Funded Futures $250M+, and Take Profit Trader $60M+ in trader payouts. We track these totals and re-verify them weekly on our payouts page. As always, the firm still has to honor its own rules, which is why we document those in detail.

What's the highest profit split on a fast-paying firm?+

FundedNext's Flex account pays 95%, the highest among the fast payers after Apex. Apex now pays a flat 100% of every approved payout, with no split. Most of the other quick payers sit at a flat 90/10 split.

Does a faster payout make a firm better?+

Not on its own. Payout speed matters, but so do the drawdown method, consistency requirements, and price. A firm that pays in four hours but runs a tight trailing drawdown can be harder to actually reach a payout on than a slower firm with forgiving rules. Compare the full picture before you decide.
