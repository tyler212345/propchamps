Ranking criteria

How we rank.

Our ranking is an editorial call, made against the same four criteria for every firm. Rule transparency and payout reliability count most, then discount value and program flexibility. Affiliate revenue is not one of them. Firms cannot pay to move up.

No star ratings

We don't publish star ratings or numeric scores. They imply a precision a rules audit can't support, so the order you see is our editorial call against these four criteria.

Rule transparency
Counts most

How clearly the firm publishes its drawdown method, payout cadence, consistency rules, and news restrictions. Hidden rules push a firm down.

Payout reliability
Counts most

Trader-reported payout speed against the firm's published cadence. Delays without communication push a firm down.

Active discount value
Also weighed

How much the CHAMP discount on file saves a trader on the most-purchased account sizes, measured on list prices.

Program flexibility
Also weighed

Number of paths to live capital (one-day pass, evaluation, instant funding) and how clearly each path is differentiated.

Data labels

Which numbers are firm-published vs PropChamps estimates

Not every number on PropChamps has the same standing. These are the five labels we use, on the payout tracker, the best-of lists and the head-to-head pages.

Firm-published

The firm's own claim

Payout totals that a firm publishes about itself, such as "$800M+ paid". We record the figure and the date we read it on the firm's site. We can't audit it.

Read at the source

Rules, caps and prices

Values read directly on the firm's help center or plan pages: drawdown, daily loss limit, consistency, payout caps and minimums, account limits, list prices.

Search-index excerpt

When a firm blocks us

If the official page blocks our check, we use a search engine's excerpt of that same page, word it as "per the firm's help center", and ask you to confirm in your dashboard.

Our reading

Arithmetic from a rule

Things the firm implies but doesn't state. Example: a 40% consistency rule means at least 3 profitable days before a payout. We label these as our reading.

PropChamps estimate

Numbers firms don't publish

Payout counts, average payout, largest single payout and median time to payout. Firms don't publish them, so ours are estimates and are always marked "est.".

About PropChamps

Why this site exists.

PropChamps started because picking a futures prop firm means comparing dozens of rules across firms whose terms change without warning. The existing comparison sites we tried were either out of date by the time we used them, or read like affiliate funnels rather than research.

Our goal: build the reference traders open when they want to know what's actually true about a firm right now. We track rules, drawdowns, payouts and promo codes, and we publish what changes, including our own corrections.

Frequently asked

Quick answers.

The questions traders ask most about how we run the audit, rank firms, and handle affiliate revenue.

How does PropChamps rank prop firms? +
Our ranking is editorial: we judge every firm against the same four criteria. Rule transparency and payout reliability count most, then active discount value and program flexibility. We don't publish a numeric score or star rating. Affiliate revenue is not a factor — firms cannot pay to be ranked higher.
How often is PropChamps updated? +
We re-audit every firm in dated passes against its own help center and site; the most recent was Oct 23, 2026. Rule changes and corrections to our own data are logged in the audit log with the date we found them.
Does PropChamps accept payment from prop firms? +
No. Firms cannot pay to be ranked higher. We earn commission from affiliate links when readers sign up, but commission rates have zero effect on the order we rank firms in.
Where does the CHAMP discount code come from? +
CHAMP is an affiliate code set up with each firm we cover. The discount shown for each firm is the rate on file, and the final price shows at checkout. If a firm's own public promo is bigger, the deals page says so.
Which numbers on PropChamps are estimates? +
Payout counts, average payouts, largest single payouts and median payout times are PropChamps estimates, because firms don't publish them. Payout totals marked firm-published are each firm's own figure (the one we haven't re-checked is marked, and one is withheld while under review), and rules, caps and prices are read on the firm's help center or site. The five labels are explained above.
What kind of prop firms does PropChamps cover? +
PropChamps focuses on futures prop trading firms based in the US, UK, and UAE — firms that publish their drawdown methods, offer transparent rules, and have a verifiable payout history.
Affiliate disclosure

How we make money.

PropChamps earns commission when readers click an affiliate link on this site and sign up with a firm. The CHAMP discount code is itself an affiliate code — using it gets you a discount, and the firm credits us a percentage of your purchase. Standard arrangement for comparison sites.

What affiliate revenue does not do:

  • Affect rankings. Firms pay zero to be ranked higher. The order comes from the four criteria above — never from commission rates.
  • Hide negative information. If a firm tightens rules or delays payouts, it goes on the page next to the affiliate link.
  • Block coverage. We list firms whose affiliate programs we are not part of when they're relevant.
In one sentence

If a firm pays us more, it doesn't move up. If a firm pays us nothing, it doesn't move down. The CHAMP code is a real discount and we earn from it — that's the entire financial relationship.
