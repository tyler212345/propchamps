Comparing futures prop firms

Which futures prop firm is best overall? +
No single firm is best for every trader, because the rules that matter most pull in different directions: drawdown type, daily loss limit, consistency rule and payout cadence. Our ranked list of all 8 futures prop firms orders them by our published criteria, and the chart above shows every plan's rules side by side.
Which futures prop firm is cheapest? +
It depends on the account size and the discount. Two examples from our data: Top One Futures' Elite Access evaluation is $39 one-time at every size (you then pay a $139 to $359 activation fee after passing), and Apex's 25K Intraday Trail evaluation lists at $167 before CHAMP's 90%-off discount on file. Our cheapest prop firm challenges list works out the cost per size.
Where does this comparison data come from? +
From each firm's own help center and website, read in dated passes; the latest was Oct 23, 2026. Prices are list prices (Lucid's are with-CHAMP prices from our Oct 5 check), and code CHAMP applies its discount at checkout.
