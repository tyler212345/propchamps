How we ranked

Cheapest means cheapest to fund.

The sticker price on a challenge is rarely the real cost. We ranked by the total to get funded after the CHAMP code: the evaluation fee plus any activation fee, and whether the plan bills monthly until you pass. A big percentage off with a steep activation charge can end up dearer than it looks, so a waived or absent activation fee counts for a lot. Discounts are applied at checkout, so confirm the final price there. Every live offer is on the deals page.

Frequently asked

Cheap challenge questions.

Which prop firm challenge is cheapest? +
On sticker price, Top One's $39 Elite Access evaluation is the cheapest entry we track, though you pay a $139 to $359 activation fee after passing. With code CHAMP, Apex's 90% off makes its evaluations the cheapest after the discount, before its activation fee. Lucid's CHAMP prices start at $50.30 with free activation. Confirm the final price at checkout.
Does the discount cover the activation fee? +
Usually not. Apex's help center says coupon codes don't apply to activation fees, so its No-Activation build is the way to skip that fee. My Funded Futures, Tradeify, FundedNext, Alpha Futures and Lucid's evaluations have no activation fee at all, while Top One Elite Access ($139 to $359) and Take Profit Trader ($130, often waived) charge one when you're funded.
Is a cheaper prop firm worse? +
Not necessarily. Price and quality are separate: Apex is both the most discounted and the firm with the largest published payout total we track. But always check the rules and payout record alongside the price, because a cheap challenge with rules you can't trade within is a false economy.
How do I get the CHAMP discount? +
Enter CHAMP at checkout on the firm's site. The discount is applied there, so confirm the final price before you pay. If the code doesn't work at a size you want, tell us at hello@propchamps.net and we'll recheck it.
