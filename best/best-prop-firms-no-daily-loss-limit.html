How we ranked

Not all "no DLL" is equal.

Some plans drop the daily loss limit at every stage; others drop it only in the evaluation, or let you switch it off at checkout. We ranked for how completely the DLL is gone, then weighed payouts and price, because a no-DLL plan that pays slowly isn't a win. Removing the daily loss limit doesn't remove the trailing drawdown, which still caps your total loss and ends most accounts.

My Funded Futures

The broadest no-DLL lineup, with no activation fee anywhere. Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro have no daily loss limit in the evaluation or once funded, and no MFF plan charges an activation fee. No daily loss limit in the Test, PRO or PRO+, on all five sizes. Frequently asked

No daily loss limit, explained.

What is a daily loss limit in prop trading? +
A daily loss limit (DLL) caps how much you can lose in one trading day. Hit it and trading stops for the day, or on a hard limit the account fails. Soft limits, like My Funded Futures Builder's, pause you for the day instead. The max drawdown is a separate cap on your total loss.
Which prop firms have no daily loss limit? +
Take Profit Trader has none at any stage or size. My Funded Futures has none on Rapid, Rapid EOD or Pro, FundedNext none on Flex, Legacy or Rapid Pro, Tradeify none on Select Flex, and Alpha Futures none on Advanced. Lucid lets you switch it off at checkout, though funded accounts add its LucidScale limit.
Is a daily loss limit the same as daily drawdown? +
Mostly, yes. Forex and CFD firms often say daily drawdown, while futures firms usually say daily loss limit; both cap one day's loss. Neither is the max (trailing) drawdown, which caps your total loss and still applies on every plan on this list.
Is trading without a daily loss limit risky? +
It gives you more freedom but puts all daily risk management on you, because there is no hard stop to save you from a runaway session. It suits disciplined traders who size correctly. You still have to respect the trailing drawdown, which is the rule that ends most accounts.
Does no daily loss limit mean no rules? +
No. Removing the daily loss limit doesn't remove the trailing drawdown, the consistency rule, minimum trading days or news restrictions. Those still apply, and the daily loss limit is just one of several rules a firm can set.
