When does the rule apply?

A consistency rule caps how much of your profit can come from your single best day. Firms apply it in the evaluation, in the funded account, or both. That is why "no consistency rule" means different things on different plans. Sources: each firm's help center, read Oct 23, 2026.

Honest catches

What replaces the consistency rule.

No firm removes the rule and adds nothing in its place. These are the limits to check before you buy.

01

Payout caps and 50% withdrawals

FundedNext Flex and Lucid Flex pay at most 50% of your profit per payout, up to a fixed cap. Rapid Daily caps each reward at $800 to $2,500 and ends after 5 rewards; Lucid Flex moves you live after 5 payouts.

02

Maximum daily profit

Lucid Daily has a max daily sim profit of $6,000 / $8,000 / $10,000 / $12,000, and reaching it moves you live. On MFF Rapid, $10,000 of net profit in one session moves you to Live and profit above $10K that day is forfeited.

03

Buffers before the first payout

Take Profit Trader PRO, Lucid Daily, Top One Elite Daily, MFF Rapid and FundedNext Rapid Daily only pay profit above a buffer, so your first withdrawal needs about the max drawdown (or more) in profit first.

04

The eval rule still decides when you pass

A plan with no funded rule still needs you to pass its eval rule: at least 3 balanced days at 40%, 4 at 30%, and 2 or more at 50%. Big single days don't count for much until the rest of your trading catches up.

05

Daily loss limits

FundedNext Rapid Daily, Tradeify Select Daily, Top One Elite Daily and Elite Access (funded) all keep a DLL. One bad session can end the day, or the account on a hard limit.

06

Rules change, and firms contradict themselves

We verified every rule on this page on Oct 23, 2026. Even so, Lucid's own articles disagree on the Lucid Daily eval rule (40% vs 50%), so read the rule in your dashboard before you trade around it.

Frequently asked

No consistency rule, answered.

Which futures prop firm has no consistency rule? +
FundedNext Rapid Daily is the only plan among the 27 we compare with no consistency rule in the challenge or the funded account. Two newer, limited plans also skip it at every stage: My Funded Futures Studio and Top One Futures X-Daily Spark. Every other plan has the rule in at least one stage.
Which prop firms have no consistency rule once you're funded? +
Twelve plans drop it after you pass: Take Profit Trader PRO and PRO+, My Funded Futures Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro, Tradeify Select (Daily and Flex), Lucid Flex and Lucid Daily, Top One Elite Daily, Alpha Futures Advanced, and FundedNext Flex and Legacy. Each still has a consistency rule in its evaluation, from 30% to 50%.
Is there an instant funding prop firm with no consistency rule? +
No. Every straight-to-funded futures plan we track keeps one: Top One Ignite 15%, Lucid Direct, Alpha Direct, Top One Instant Sim Funded and FundedNext Direct 20%, and Tradeify Lightning 20% rising to 30% by the third payout.
Which prop firm has no consistency rule and daily payouts? +
Among the 27 plans we compare, FundedNext Rapid Daily is the only one with daily payouts and no consistency rule at any stage; Top One's limited X-Daily Spark (50K only, 80/20 split) also pays daily with no rule. If you can live with a rule in the evaluation, Take Profit Trader PRO, My Funded Futures Rapid and Rapid EOD, Tradeify Select Daily, Lucid Daily and Top One Elite Daily all pay daily with no consistency rule once funded.
Is a plan with no consistency rule easier to get paid on? +
Not automatically. Plans without the rule usually limit you another way: a cap on each payout, a 50%-of-profit withdrawal limit, a buffer you must clear first, a daily loss limit, or a maximum daily profit. Compare those before you pick a plan just because it has no consistency rule.
