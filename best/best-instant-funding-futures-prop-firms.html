Quick answer

Which futures prop firms offer instant funding?

Six plans at five firms: Lucid Direct, Tradeify Lightning, Alpha Futures Direct, Top One Instant Sim Funded and Top One Ignite, and the new FundedNext Direct (50K only). Apex, My Funded Futures and Take Profit Trader don't sell a straight-to-funded account.

None of them is free of a consistency rule: limits run from 15% (Ignite) to 20% on most and 20% → 30% on Lightning. Most also need a first payout goal of $1,500 to $9,000 by size, so "instant" describes the funding, not the payout.

Instant vs evaluation

What you trade for skipping the eval.

Instant funding with no consistency rule?

None of the six. With no evaluation to filter traders, every instant plan leans on the rule, from 15% (Ignite) to 30% (Lightning from payout 3). If you want no rule while you prove yourself, look at evaluations with none: Top One Elite Access ($39) or FundedNext Rapid Pro, both passable in a day. See the plans with no consistency rule.

Instant funding with daily payouts?

None of the six pays daily. Each needs a profit goal or a buffer before every payout; Tradeify markets Lightning as 5-day payouts and Alpha Direct allows up to four a month. The daily-payout plans are all evaluations, listed on the fastest payout futures prop firms page.

The cost difference

At 50K, the instant plans cost $169.99 to $679 in our data. Evaluations at the same size start far lower (Top One's $39 Elite Access eval, plus activation after passing). You're paying to skip the pass, not for an easier payout.

Who instant funding suits

Traders who already trade consistently, size small relative to the max loss, and would rather pay once than retry an evaluation. For the concept in depth, read instant funding vs evaluation.

Frequently asked

Instant funding questions.

Which futures prop firms offer instant funding? +
Five of the eight firms we track sell a straight-to-funded futures account: Lucid Trading (Direct), Tradeify (Lightning), Alpha Futures (Direct), Top One Futures (Instant Sim Funded and Ignite) and FundedNext (Direct, 50K only). Apex, My Funded Futures and Take Profit Trader don't.
Is there an instant funding futures prop firm with no consistency rule? +
No. Every instant plan we track has one: Top One Ignite 15%; Lucid Direct, Alpha Direct, Top One Instant Sim Funded and FundedNext Direct 20%; and Tradeify Lightning 20%, then 25% and 30% from the second and third payouts.
What is the cheapest instant funding futures prop firm? +
FundedNext Direct was $169.99 at our Oct 23, 2026 check, but it comes only in 50K. Among plans sold in four sizes, the lowest 25K list price is Top One Ignite at $218; Lucid Direct 25K was $230.30 with code CHAMP at our Oct 5 checkout check. Codes change the order: Alpha Direct 25K is $349 list, $209.40 with CHAMP's 40% on file or $174.50 with Alpha's public MYALPHA50 code (50% off Direct at our Oct 23 check), by our math. Prices and promos change often, so confirm at checkout.
Do instant funding prop firms offer daily payouts? +
Not on the instant plans we track. Each one makes you reach a profit goal or clear a buffer before every payout, and Tradeify markets Lightning as 5-day payouts. If daily payouts matter more than skipping the evaluation, look at the daily-payout plans on our fastest payout list.
Is instant funding better than an evaluation? +
It's faster to start, not easier to get paid. You pay more up front, every instant plan keeps a consistency rule of 15% to 30%, and most need a first payout goal of $1,500 to $9,000 by size. An evaluation usually costs less, and some have no consistency rule while you pass.
