Fastest payout futures prop firms.

Two things decide how fast you get paid: how soon and how often a plan lets you request a payout, and how long the firm takes to process it. Here are the seven daily-payout plans, when each firm's first payout opens, estimated processing times and our top picks.

Quick answer

Which futures prop firm pays out the fastest?

It depends which kind of fast you mean. For how often you can withdraw, seven plans let you request daily: Take Profit Trader PRO, My Funded Futures Rapid and Rapid EOD, Lucid Daily, Tradeify Select Daily, Top One Elite Daily and FundedNext Rapid Daily.

For processing, My Funded Futures says most payouts are approved instantly, and Take Profit Trader's account-to-wallet transfers on Tradovate PRO accounts take 5 to 10 seconds. By our estimates (firms don't publish them), Apex pays in about 4 hours and Top One in under 12. Apex and Alpha Futures don't offer daily payouts.

Processing times

Payout speed has two parts.

When a firm advertises "fast payouts", it usually means one of two things, and they're rarely the same:

  • Eligibility and frequency: how soon after funding, and how often, you're allowed to request a withdrawal. That can be daily, every 5 trading days, or after a benchmark cycle. The tables above cover this.
  • Processing time: how long the money takes to reach you once the request is in. The table below covers this.

A firm can be good at one and slow at the other. Take Profit Trader lets PRO traders request daily, but a bank withdrawal still goes through a review of up to 12 business hours. Apex gates requests behind 5 qualifying days, yet pays in about four hours by our estimate. Decide which one you care about before you compare.

The real delay

What actually delays your payout.

It's rarely the bank transfer. These rules decide when you see money, and none of them show up in a "paid in hours" headline.

01

Minimum days per payout

Apex (5 qualifying days), Alpha (5 winning days of $200+), Top One Elite Access (5 profitable days), Lucid Flex (5 profit days) and FundedNext Flex and Legacy (5 benchmark days) all make you trade for days before each request.

02

Consistency rules

A cap on how much of your profit can come from one day, often 20% to 50%. Hit your goal in one big session and you keep trading until the rest catches up. See the plans with no consistency rule.

03

Buffers and profit goals

Every daily-payout plan only pays profit above a buffer, usually about the size of the max drawdown. Instant accounts need a first profit goal of $1,500 to $9,000 by size.

04

Minimums and caps

Stated minimum requests run from $200 (Alpha Zero) to $1,000 or more (Tradeify Lightning, MFF Pro and Alpha Advanced; MFF Builder 150K needs $1,500). Take Profit Trader states none, but charges $50 on wallet withdrawals of $250 or less. Caps per request decide how much of a big week you can take out at once.

Frequently asked

Fast payout questions.

Which futures prop firm pays out the fastest? +
For how often you can withdraw, seven plans let you request daily: Take Profit Trader PRO, My Funded Futures Rapid and Rapid EOD, Lucid Daily, Tradeify Select Daily, Top One Elite Daily and FundedNext Rapid Daily. For processing, My Funded Futures says most payouts are approved instantly, and by our estimates Apex pays in about 4 hours and Top One in under 12. Firms don't publish processing times, so treat those medians as estimates.
Is there a prop firm with same-day payouts? +
Yes, on some plans. My Funded Futures says most payouts are approved instantly, with manual reviews taking 6 to 12 business hours, and Take Profit Trader's account-to-wallet transfers on Tradovate PRO accounts are automated and take 5 to 10 seconds; bank withdrawals then go through a review of up to 12 business hours. Both firms also let you request daily on their fastest plans.
Which futures prop firms offer daily payouts? +
Seven plans: Take Profit Trader PRO (from day one, no maximum), My Funded Futures Rapid and Rapid EOD ($500 minimum), Lucid Daily (no per-payout cap above the buffer), Tradeify Select Daily ($600 to $2,500 cap), Top One Elite Daily ($750 to $2,250 cap) and FundedNext Rapid Daily ($800 to $2,500 cap). Each pays only profit above a buffer.
How long do prop firm payouts take? +
By our estimates, most firms we track pay within about a day of approval. Lucid says approved funds are sent within 2 business days, FundedNext says about 99.99% of payouts are processed within 24 hours, and My Funded Futures' manual reviews take 6 to 12 business hours. The longer wait is usually eligibility: minimum days, buffers and consistency rules decide when you can request at all.
Do Apex and Alpha Futures offer daily payouts? +
No. Apex pays after every 5 qualifying trading days, up to 6 payouts per PA, per its help center. Alpha Futures allows up to 4 withdrawals a month after 5 winning days of $200 or more, except Direct, which uses a profit target and a 20% consistency rule instead.
What's the highest profit split on a fast-paying firm? +
Apex pays 100% of every approved payout, with no split. FundedNext Flex pays 95%, and most other plans pay 90/10. Take Profit Trader PRO is 80/20, rising to 90/10 on the live PRO+ account.
Do fast payouts mean a firm is reliable? +
Read speed and reliability together. A quick median backed by a large published payout total, like Apex's $800M+ or My Funded Futures' $250M+, means more than a fast time on a small record. Our medians are estimates, so use them as a guide rather than a promise.
