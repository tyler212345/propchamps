Which prop firm pays the most?

By the totals the firms publish themselves, Apex Trader Funding has paid out the most: $800M+. Next come FundedNext ($380M+, across all of FundedNext's programs, not just futures), Tradeify ($350M+), My Funded Futures ($250M+), Alpha Futures ($70M+) and Top One Futures ($30M+). Take Profit Trader reports $60M+, which we haven't re-checked yet. Lucid Trading's total is under review.

Per payout, the split decides how much of each withdrawal you keep. 20 of the 27 plans we track pay the trader 90%; the exceptions are 100% on Apex Intraday Trail and Apex EOD Trail, 95% on FundedNext Flex and 80% on Take Profit Trader PRO, My Funded Futures Pro, My Funded Futures Builder Plan and FundedNext Legacy.

A big total mostly reflects how large and how old a firm is. It doesn't tell you how likely you are to get paid, or how quickly. For that, read the rules: how often you can request, the minimum and the cap per request, and the consistency rule that can pause a payout.

How to read a payout tracker

Totals are the firm's claim. The rules are what you sign.

A payout total is a firm's own number, and we can't audit it. We record it with the date we read it on the firm's site. What decides whether you get paid is the rulebook for the plan you buy, so the table above puts the request cadence and the split next to every total.

Cadence and caps decide your first payout

Most plans cap each request and set a minimum. Apex, for example, caps the first payout at $1,000 to $2,500 depending on account size and needs at least $500, and Lucid and Top One's Elite plans also have a $500 minimum. A daily-payout plan with a small cap can pay you sooner but in smaller pieces than a plan that pays every few days.

Consistency rules can pause a payout

Many funded accounts limit how much of your profit can come from a single day: 40% on Lucid Pro and on Alpha's qualified Zero and Standard accounts, 50% at Apex. At Alpha, going over pauses the payout until more profitable days bring you back under the limit; it doesn't fail the account. Apex's help center says the same of its rule (Oct 2026, read through a search-index excerpt because Apex blocked our check), so confirm in your dashboard.

Simulated accounts, real withdrawals

Most funded accounts at these firms trade in a simulated environment, but approved payouts are real withdrawals. Some plans lead to a live account: Take Profit Trader's PRO+, the Live account at Blue Row Capital after five MFF Builder payouts, and Lucid's live tier.

It depends on the plan more than the firm. 7 of the 27 plans we track allow daily requests: Tradeify Select — Daily Path, Lucid Trading Daily, Take Profit Trader PRO, Top One Futures Elite Daily, My Funded Futures Rapid, My Funded Futures Rapid EOD and FundedNext Rapid — Daily.
