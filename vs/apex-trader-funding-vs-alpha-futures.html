Apex Trader Funding vs Alpha Futures

One is the discount king; the other sells freedom. Apex runs the biggest coupon and now keeps 100% of your payout; Alpha's Advanced plan drops the daily loss limit and news restrictions entirely. Here's how they stack up.

Jump to The verdict Full comparison Which to pick FAQ
The bottom line

The short version.

Apex Trader Funding wins on price and payout keep: CHAMP takes 90% off, funded accounts keep a flat 100% (no split), and the payout history is enormous. You get a 1-day pass and a choice of Intraday or EOD trailing drawdown — but funded payouts are capped on a six-payout ladder.

Alpha Futures is the premium pick. Its Advanced plan pays 90% from day one, carries no daily loss limit and no news restrictions, and allows $15,000-per-request withdrawals; its Zero plan offers a genuine one-day pass. Alpha keeps a flat 90% split across the board and uses an end-of-day trailing Maximum Loss Limit. CHAMP's discount at Alpha is smaller than Apex's.

In one line

Want the cheapest entry and a 100% payout? Apex. Want to trade news with no daily loss limit and take big withdrawals? Alpha Advanced.

Frequently asked

Apex Trader Funding vs Alpha Futures — quick answers.

Which has the bigger discount? +
Apex — CHAMP takes 90% off the evaluation, the biggest standing discount on the site. Alpha also honors CHAMP but at a smaller rate; confirm both on the deals page.
Which pays more of your profit? +
Apex now pays a flat 100% (no split) but caps each payout on a six-payout ladder. Alpha pays a flat 90% with no payout-count cap. Which nets more depends on how much and how often you withdraw.
Which is better for news trading? +
Alpha Futures — its Advanced plan has no news restrictions. Apex allows trading through news with a normal strategy but prohibits dedicated news/straddle strategies and hedging.
How do the drawdowns compare? +
Apex offers a tighter Intraday Trail (no eval DLL) or EOD Trail (with a DLL). Alpha uses an end-of-day trailing Maximum Loss Limit; its Advanced plan carries no daily loss limit at all.
