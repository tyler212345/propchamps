The bottom line

The short version.

Take Profit Trader is the minimalist's pick: no daily loss limit at any stage, one TEST, then PRO and PRO+. PRO pays 80/20 from day one above a buffer, with no funded consistency rule and no maximum withdrawal; PRO+ is a live-market account at 90/10. The TEST is billed monthly ($150 to $360) and takes at least 3 trading days, plus a $130 activation fee that promos often waive.

My Funded Futures is the maximalist's pick: four one-time plans with no activation fee, no DLL on Rapid, Rapid EOD or Pro, and daily Rapid payouts at 90/10 with no stated cap. Rapid trails intraday once funded, much like TPT PRO; Rapid EOD stays end-of-day. Builder's five sim payouts unlock a real live account at Blue Row Capital. MFF publishes a $250M+ payout total; TPT publishes $60M+.

In one line

Want the simplest rulebook and day-one withdrawals with no cap? Take Profit Trader. Want one-time pricing, a 90/10 daily plan and a ladder to live? My Funded Futures.

  • BillingTPT: monthly until you pass, plus $130 activation (often waived). MFF: one-time, no activation fee.
  • Daily loss limitTPT: none anywhere. MFF: none on Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro; Builder soft pause.
  • SplitTPT: 80/20 on PRO, 90/10 on PRO+ (live). MFF: 90/10 on Rapid and Rapid EOD, 80/20 on Pro and Builder.
  • Daily payoutsTPT PRO: from day one, no maximum. MFF Rapid and Rapid EOD: no stated cap, $500 minimum.
  • Funded drawdownBoth trail intraday on their main funded account (TPT PRO, MFF Rapid). TPT PRO+ and MFF Rapid EOD, Pro and Builder are end-of-day.
  • NewsBoth restrict funded accounts: TPT flat 1 minute either side of FOMC, NFP and CPI; MFF no Tier-1 news 2 minutes either side.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: the TPT ladder vs MFF

Take Profit Trader sells one evaluation in five sizes; MFF sells four plans.

TPT TEST and PRO vs MFF Rapid

The main paths

The TEST needs 3 trading days and 50% consistency; Rapid needs 2 days and 50%. Neither has a DLL or a funded consistency rule, and both trail intraday once funded. TPT PRO pays 80/20 with no maximum once you clear a buffer equal to the max drawdown; Rapid pays 90/10 daily with no stated cap once you clear a $1,100 to $4,600 buffer.

TPT PRO vs MFF Rapid EOD

Drawdown once funded

If intraday trailing worries you, Rapid EOD (25K and 50K) keeps end-of-day drawdown when funded and still pays daily with no cap per payout; its evaluation takes 4 days with 30% consistency. TPT PRO trails intraday until it reaches the starting balance.

TPT PRO+ vs MFF Builder

The live step

PRO+ is TPT's live account (90/10, end-of-day, no buffer), reached by a holistic review. Builder leads to a real live account at Blue Row Capital after 5 approved sim payouts (80/20, end-of-day, with a live DLL).

TPT PRO vs MFF Pro

High ceiling

MFF Pro pays 80/20 every 14 calendar days with a $1,000 minimum and up to $100K in sim payouts per user. TPT PRO pays the same 80/20, but from day one, with no maximum.

After the sim

Path to a live account

Take Profit Trader PRO+. Promotion from PRO is a holistic review, not a fixed threshold. PRO+ is a live-market account paying 90/10 with end-of-day drawdown and no buffer, starting at a $0 balance with the original drawdown; $5,000 of your PRO profit stays frozen while PRO+ is active.

My Funded Futures. Builder: 5 approved sim payouts unlock a real live account at Blue Row Capital (daily payouts, 80/20, end-of-day drawdown, a live DLL, no consistency rule). Pro: 3 consecutive payouts or $20,000 of profit triggers a Live review. Rapid: a $10,000 net-profit session moves you Live automatically, or the risk team can review you.

Fine print

News rules and account limits

News. Both restrict funded accounts. TPT: the TEST is unrestricted; PRO and PRO+ must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI. MFF: Tier-1 news is banned on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after; it's allowed in evaluations and on Builder.

Accounts. TPT: up to 5 active funded accounts per person (PRO and PRO+ combined), unlimited tests, up to 10 passed-test activations per 30 days, approved copiers only. MFF: up to 10 evaluations and 5 sim-funded accounts if all are 25K or 50K, 3 if any is 100K or 150K; copy trading allowed across your own accounts.

Frequently asked

Take Profit Trader vs My Funded Futures: quick answers.

Do both remove the daily loss limit? +
Take Profit Trader has none at any size or stage. My Funded Futures has none on Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro; Builder has a soft pause ($600 to $2,500), and Builder's live account has a DLL.
Which is cheaper with code CHAMP? +
TPT only if you pass in the first month and the activation fee is waived: a 50K TEST is $102 after 40% off, plus a $130 activation fee unless a promo covers it. MFF Builder 50K lists at $153 and Rapid at $209 before CHAMP's up-to-50% discount, paid once, with no activation fee. Each extra month at TPT adds $102.
Which pays out faster? +
Both pay daily: TPT PRO from day one above the buffer, MFF Rapid from 24 hours after your first funded trade. MFF says most payouts are approved instantly; TPT's wallet transfers on Tradovate PRO take seconds and bank withdrawals up to 12 business hours.
Can you trade news on Take Profit Trader or MFF? +
Not freely on funded accounts at either. TPT PRO and PRO+ must be flat 1 minute either side of FOMC, NFP and CPI; MFF bans Tier-1 news on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts, 2 minutes either side. Both allow news in the evaluation, and MFF allows it on Builder.
Which offers bigger accounts? +
Both go to 150K. TPT also has a 75K size; MFF's Rapid EOD stops at 50K. MFF allows 5 sim-funded accounts (3 if any is 100K or 150K); TPT allows 5 funded accounts across PRO and PRO+.
