Lucid Trading vs Take Profit Trader

Take Profit Trader has no daily loss limit at any stage and no maximum withdrawal on PRO; Lucid has four plan types, a 90/10 split and free activation on its evaluations. Here's how the evaluations, payouts and costs compare.

The bottom line

The short version.

Lucid Trading gives you more choice: four paths (Pro, Flex, Daily and Direct), end-of-day drawdown on Pro, Flex and Direct, an optional daily loss limit, a 90/10 split from the first payout and free activation on its evaluations. Its payouts are capped on Pro, Flex and Direct ($1,000 to $3,500 per payout depending on plan, size and payout number); LucidDaily has no per-payout cap above the buffer.

Take Profit Trader has one path: TEST, then PRO, then PRO+, with no daily loss limit at any stage. PRO lets you withdraw from day one once your balance clears a buffer equal to the max drawdown, with no funded consistency rule and no maximum withdrawal, at 80/20; PRO+ is a live account at 90/10. The catches: the TEST is billed monthly ($150 to $360 by size at our Oct 5 audit) until you pass, there's a $130 activation fee (often waived by promo), PRO's drawdown trails intraday, and PRO and PRO+ must be flat around FOMC, NFP and CPI.

In one line

Want no daily loss limit and no payout cap, and fine with monthly billing? Take Profit Trader. Want 90/10 from your first payout, plan choice and free activation? Lucid.

  • Daily loss limitTPT: none, at any size or stage. Lucid: optional on Pro, Flex and Daily; Direct has one from 50K.
  • Payout capTPT PRO: none. Lucid: $1,000 to $3,500 per payout on Pro and Direct, 50% of profit up to $3,000 on Flex, none above the buffer on Daily.
  • SplitLucid: 90/10 everywhere. TPT: 80/20 on PRO, 90/10 on PRO+ (live).
  • BillingTPT: monthly until you pass, plus $130 activation (often waived). Lucid: CHAMP prices from $50.30 at our Oct 5 checkout check, free activation.
  • Funded drawdownTPT PRO: intraday trailing (stops at the starting balance); PRO+ end-of-day. Lucid Pro, Flex and Direct: end-of-day; Daily intraday.
  • Minimum daysTPT TEST: at least 3 trading days (tests bought from Aug 17, 2026). Lucid Pro: 1-day pass.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: Lucid's four paths vs the TPT ladder

Take Profit Trader sells one evaluation in five sizes (25K, 50K, 75K, 100K and 150K); Lucid sells four plan types in four sizes. These are the closest matches.

Lucid Pro vs TPT TEST and PRO

Evaluation to first payout

Pro has no consistency rule in the evaluation and can pass in a day; the TPT TEST checks 50% consistency and needs at least 3 trading days. Drawdowns match at 50K, 100K and 150K; TPT gives more at 25K ($1,500 vs $1,000). Once funded, Pro checks 40% consistency and caps each payout; TPT PRO checks none and has no maximum.

LucidDaily vs TPT PRO

Daily payouts

Both let you request daily and both trail intraday once funded. LucidDaily has no per-payout cap above its buffer but moves you live at the max daily sim profit ($6K to $12K); TPT PRO has no maximum withdrawal at all, pays 80/20, and charges $50 on wallet withdrawals of $250 or less.

Lucid Flex vs TPT

No consistency rule once funded

Lucid Flex checks 40% consistency in the evaluation and none funded, like TPT (50% on the TEST, none on PRO). Flex pays 50% of profit, up to $1,000 to $3,000, after 5 profit days, at 90/10; TPT PRO pays any amount above the buffer at 80/20.

Lucid Direct

No evaluation

Lucid Direct starts funded, at a one-time price ($230.30 to $585.20 with CHAMP at our Oct 5 checkout check), with 20% consistency and capped payouts. Take Profit Trader has no straight-to-funded plan.

Getting paid

Lucid vs Take Profit Trader payout rules

Which pays out faster? Take Profit Trader PRO lets you withdraw from day one once the buffer is cleared, with no profit-day minimum and no cap. Transfers from the account to your TPT wallet on Tradovate PRO accounts are automated (5 to 10 seconds); a bank withdrawal then goes through a manual review of up to 12 business hours before Plaid, PayPal or Wise. LucidDaily also pays daily; Lucid's other plans pay on request once you're eligible, and approved funds are sent within 2 business days.

After the sim

Path to a live account

Take Profit Trader. PRO+ is a live-market account paying 90/10 with end-of-day drawdown and no buffer. It starts at a $0 balance with the original drawdown, and $5,000 of your PRO profit stays frozen while PRO+ is active. Promotion from PRO is a holistic review rather than a fixed threshold.

Lucid. Pro and Direct accounts join the live review pool at payout 5, Flex moves you live after up to 5 payouts, and LucidDaily moves you live when you hit the max daily sim profit. Lucid Live accounts pay daily with end-of-day drawdown, no DLL and no consistency rule.

Fine print

News, trading hours and account limits

News. The TPT TEST is unrestricted; PRO and PRO+ must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI. Lucid Pro, Flex and Direct allow trading around news; LucidDaily makes red-folder news a hard breach on funded accounts. Lucid also requires positions flat by 4:45 PM EST.

Accounts. TPT allows up to 5 active funded accounts per person (PRO and PRO+ combined), unlimited tests and up to 10 passed-test activations per 30 days; approved trade copiers can be used across your own accounts. Lucid allows up to 10 evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household across all its plan types (no more than 10 in total), and permits trade copiers.

Frequently asked

Lucid Trading vs Take Profit Trader: quick answers.

Is Take Profit Trader or Lucid better? +
If the daily loss limit and payout caps are what bother you, Take Profit Trader: it has no DLL anywhere and no maximum withdrawal on PRO, though PRO pays 80/20 and the TEST bills monthly. If you would rather keep 90% from the first payout, pick from four plan types and pay no activation fee, Lucid.
Which is cheaper, Take Profit Trader or Lucid? +
It depends on how fast you pass. Lucid Pro 50K was $115.40 with CHAMP at our Oct 5 checkout check, with free activation. TPT's 50K TEST lists at $170 a month; at our Oct 23, 2026 check, TPT's public code NOFEE40 took 40% off ($102 a month) and waived the $130 activation fee. Pass in the first month and TPT is cheaper; from the second month ($204) Lucid is. CHAMP also works at TPT's checkout, but we couldn't confirm its rate.
Does Take Profit Trader have a daily loss limit? +
No. TPT has none at any size or stage (TEST, PRO and PRO+). Lucid's DLL is optional on Pro, Flex and Daily, and Direct has one from 50K.
Is there a payout cap at Take Profit Trader? +
Not on PRO: once your balance clears the buffer (equal to the max drawdown) you can withdraw any amount, from day one. Lucid caps Pro and Direct at $1,000 to $3,500 per payout and Flex at 50% of profit up to $3,000; LucidDaily has no per-payout cap above the buffer.
Take Profit Trader vs Lucid Flex: which is better? +
Lucid Flex checks 40% consistency in the evaluation (50% with an add-on) and none funded, then pays 50% of profit (up to $1,000 to $3,000) after 5 profit days, at 90/10. TPT checks 50% on the TEST and none on PRO, then pays any amount above the buffer at 80/20. Flex suits traders who want the higher split; TPT suits traders who want no cap.
Can you trade news on Take Profit Trader or Lucid? +
On TPT, during the TEST yes; on PRO and PRO+ you must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI. On Lucid Pro, Flex and Direct yes; on LucidDaily, red-folder news is a hard breach once funded. See news trading rules by firm.
