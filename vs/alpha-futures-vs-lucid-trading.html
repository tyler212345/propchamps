Alpha Futures vs Lucid Trading

Both have a one-day evaluation, a straight-to-funded plan and a 90% split. Alpha bills its evaluations monthly and allows up to four withdrawals a month; Lucid pays on request or daily, with free activation.

The bottom line

The short version.

Alpha Futures has the widest spread of payout caps. Zero passes in a day (no evaluation consistency rule) and caps each withdrawal at $1,000 to $2,500; Standard caps at $3,000 to $5,000; Advanced allows up to $15,000 per request, with no daily loss limit at any stage and no news restrictions. Zero, Standard and Advanced withdraw up to 50% of profit, up to 4 times a month, after 5 winning days of $200 or more. Evaluations are monthly subscriptions that stop when you pass, with $0 activation; CHAMP is 40% off in our deal data.

Lucid Trading pays 90/10 on every path and doesn't count winning days on Pro or Direct: you request once the profit goal and consistency rule are met (Pro also needs your balance above a buffer). LucidDaily pays daily with no per-payout cap above the buffer. Lucid's evaluations have free activation and an optional DLL, and its CHAMP prices at our Oct 5 checkout check started at $50.30 (Flex 25K). Its caps top out at $3,500 per payout on Pro and Direct.

In one line

Want large single withdrawals ($15,000 on Advanced) and no DLL or news limits? Alpha Advanced. Want daily payouts or no winning-day rule, with free activation? Lucid.

  • BillingAlpha Zero, Standard and Advanced: monthly until you pass ($0 activation); Direct one-time. Lucid: CHAMP prices from $50.30 at our Oct 5 checkout check; free activation on the evaluations.
  • One-day passAlpha Zero (25K to 100K) and Lucid Pro (25K to 150K); neither has an evaluation consistency rule.
  • Payout timingAlpha: up to 4 withdrawals a month after 5 winning days of $200+ (Direct uses a profit target). Lucid: on request once eligible; LucidDaily daily.
  • Biggest withdrawalAlpha Advanced: $15,000 per request. Lucid: $3,500 (Pro and Direct, 150K); LucidDaily no per-payout cap above the buffer.
  • Daily loss limitAlpha Advanced: none at any stage; Zero $500 to $2,000 at both stages. Lucid: optional on Pro, Flex and Daily.
  • AccountsAlpha: 5 qualified on Zero, Standard or Direct, 3 if you hold any Advanced; one person per household. Lucid: 5 funded per household.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: Alpha Zero, Standard, Advanced and Direct vs Lucid

Both firms sell four plans. Three pair up closely; Alpha Standard and LucidDaily have no direct counterpart.

Alpha Zero vs Lucid Pro

One-day passes

Neither checks consistency in the evaluation. Zero has a DLL at both stages ($500 to $2,000) and covers 25K to 100K; Pro's DLL is optional and it goes to 150K. Funded, both check 40% consistency. Zero caps each withdrawal at $1,000 to $2,500 ($200 minimum) after 5 winning days of $200+; Pro caps at $1,000 to $3,000 for the first payout and $1,500 to $3,500 after, with a $500 minimum and a per-cycle profit goal instead of winning days.

Alpha Advanced vs Lucid Flex

No consistency rule once funded

Both check 40% in the evaluation and none funded. Advanced has no DLL at all, no news restrictions and a $15,000 cap per request ($1,000 minimum), but needs at least 3 evaluation days and costs $209 to $489 a month. Lucid Flex makes the DLL optional and pays 50% of profit, up to $1,000 to $3,000, after 5 profit days.

Alpha Direct vs Lucid Direct

No evaluation

Both start funded with 20% consistency, the same first profit target ($1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000) and the same early caps ($1,000 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000). With CHAMP's 40% (on file), Alpha Direct works out cheaper at every size (our math) than Lucid Direct's CHAMP price at our Oct 5 checkout check, and Alpha's public MYALPHA50 code (50% off Direct at our Oct 23 check) would take it lower still. Lucid's caps rise for payouts 4 and 5; Alpha's later profit targets are lower at 25K and 50K but higher at 100K and 150K.

Alpha Standard and LucidDaily

No direct match

Alpha Standard (50K to 150K) checks 50% in the evaluation and 40% qualified, adds a DLL only once qualified ($1,000 to $3,000) and caps withdrawals at $3,000 to $5,000. LucidDaily is Lucid's daily-payout plan; Alpha has nothing that pays daily, since every Alpha plan allows up to 4 withdrawals a month.

Getting paid

Fine print

News rules, account limits and copy trading

News. Alpha has no news restrictions in any evaluation or on Advanced qualified accounts. On Zero, Standard and Direct qualified accounts you may hold positions through news, but no orders may execute within 2 minutes either side of a red-folder event; a first violation is a warning, later ones breach. Lucid Pro, Flex and Direct allow trading around news; LucidDaily makes red-folder news a hard breach on funded accounts.

Accounts. Alpha allows 5 qualified accounts on Zero, Standard (3 if bought before Jul 27, 2026) or Direct, but any active Advanced account caps you at 3, and only one individual per household. Lucid allows up to 10 evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household across every Lucid type (no more than 10 in total). Both allow copying trades across your own accounts; Alpha prohibits automated, group and reverse trading, while Lucid permits automated systems and trade copiers.

Frequently asked

Alpha Futures vs Lucid Trading: quick answers.

Is Alpha Futures or Lucid Trading better? +
For big, infrequent withdrawals, Alpha Advanced ($15,000 per request, no DLL, no news restrictions). For frequent payouts, Lucid: LucidDaily pays daily and Pro and Direct have no fixed window. On the one-day passes, Alpha Zero and Lucid Pro are close; Pro goes up to 150K and makes the DLL optional.
Which is cheaper, Alpha Futures or Lucid? +
It depends on how fast you pass. A 50K Alpha Zero is $139 a month list, or $83.40 after 40% off (CHAMP's rate on file; Alpha's public MYALPHA40 also took 40% off Zero at our Oct 23 check); Lucid Pro 50K was $115.40 with CHAMP at our Oct 5 checkout check. Pass Alpha in the first month and it's cheaper; a second month ($166.80) isn't. On the no-evaluation plans, Alpha Direct 50K is $519 list: $311.40 with CHAMP's 40% on file, or $259.50 with Alpha's public MYALPHA50 code (50% off at our Oct 23 check), against $360.50 for Lucid Direct with CHAMP at our Oct 5 checkout check.
Do Alpha Futures and Lucid both have a one-day pass? +
Yes. Alpha Zero has no minimum evaluation days and no evaluation consistency rule (25K to 100K). Lucid Pro also has no evaluation consistency rule (25K to 150K). Both then check 40% consistency once funded.
Which allows bigger withdrawals, Alpha or Lucid? +
Alpha Advanced, at up to $15,000 per request ($1,000 minimum). Lucid caps Pro and Direct at $3,500 per payout on 150K; LucidDaily has no per-payout cap above the buffer, but hitting the max daily sim profit ($6K to $12K) moves the account live.
How many accounts can you have at Alpha and Lucid? +
Alpha: 5 qualified accounts on Zero, Standard or Direct, or 3 if you hold any Advanced account, with one individual per household. Lucid: up to 5 funded accounts per household (all types combined) and up to 10 evaluations, with no more than 10 accounts in total.
Is Alpha Futures monthly? +
Zero, Standard and Advanced are monthly subscriptions that stop once you pass; qualified accounts carry no monthly fee and activation is $0. Alpha Direct is a one-time payment.
