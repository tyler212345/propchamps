Tradeify vs Take Profit Trader

Tradeify charges once with no activation fee and has a daily-payout plan; Take Profit Trader has no daily loss limit anywhere and pays daily from day one on PRO, but bills monthly. Here's how they compare.

The bottom line

The short version.

Tradeify is one-time on every plan, with no activation fee. Its Select evaluation has no daily loss limit; after passing you choose Daily payouts (capped at $600 to $2,500, with a funded DLL) or Flex (no DLL, capped at $1,250 to $4,500 on a 5-day cycle). Growth passes in a day with no evaluation consistency rule but has a DLL, and Lightning skips the evaluation. Tradeify publishes a $350M+ payout total.

Take Profit Trader keeps it simple: one TEST, then PRO, then PRO+, with no daily loss limit at any stage. PRO pays 80/20 from day one once your balance clears a buffer equal to the max drawdown, with no funded consistency rule and no maximum withdrawal; PRO+ is a live account at 90/10. The TEST is billed monthly ($150 to $360) until you pass, plus a $130 activation fee that promos often waive. TPT publishes a $60M+ payout total.

In one line

Want one-time pricing and the choice of daily or Flex payouts? Tradeify. Want no daily loss limit anywhere and no payout cap? Take Profit Trader.

  • BillingTradeify: one-time, no activation fee. TPT: monthly until you pass, plus $130 activation (often waived).
  • Daily loss limitTPT: none anywhere. Tradeify: none on the Select evaluation or Select Flex; Growth, Select Daily (funded) and Lightning (50K+) have one.
  • Daily payoutsTradeify Select Daily: $600 to $2,500 per payout. TPT PRO: no maximum, from day one.
  • SplitTradeify: 90/10. TPT: 80/20 on PRO, 90/10 on PRO+.
  • Funded drawdownTradeify: end-of-day. TPT PRO: intraday (stops at the starting balance); PRO+ end-of-day.
  • SizesTradeify: 25K to 150K. TPT adds a 75K size.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: Tradeify Growth, Select and Lightning vs the TPT ladder

Take Profit Trader sells one evaluation in five sizes; Tradeify sells three plans, with Select splitting into Daily and Flex after you pass.

Tradeify Select vs TPT TEST

No-DLL evaluations

Neither has a daily loss limit in the evaluation, and both trail end-of-day. Select checks 40% consistency and needs 3 days; the TPT TEST checks 50% and also needs 3 trading days (for tests bought from Aug 17, 2026). TPT gives more drawdown at 25K ($1,500 vs $1,000) and the same at 50K, 100K and 150K.

Tradeify Select Daily vs TPT PRO

Daily payouts

Both pay daily. Select Daily caps each payout at $600 to $2,500, adds a funded DLL ($500 to $1,750) and keeps end-of-day drawdown, at 90/10. TPT PRO has no cap and no DLL, but pays 80/20, trails intraday, and charges $50 on wallet withdrawals of $250 or less.

Tradeify Growth vs TPT TEST

Fastest pass

Growth has a 1-day minimum and no evaluation consistency rule, but a DLL ($600 to $3,750) and a 35% funded rule. The TPT TEST takes at least 3 days with 50% consistency; after that, PRO has no consistency rule to watch, only the buffer and the news rule.

Tradeify Lightning

No evaluation

Lightning starts funded ($345 to $796 list, one-time), with consistency rising from 20% to 30% and capped payouts every 5 days. Take Profit Trader has no straight-to-funded plan.

Getting paid

Tradeify vs Take Profit Trader payout rules

Which pays out faster? On cadence, both offer daily payouts: Tradeify through Select Daily, and TPT on every PRO account from day one. Tradeify's other plans run on a 5-day cycle. At TPT, account-to-wallet transfers on Tradovate PRO accounts are automated (5 to 10 seconds) and bank withdrawals take up to 12 business hours of review. Our estimated median payout times (PropChamps estimates, not firm data) are about 1 day for TPT and 5 days for Tradeify.

Fine print

News rules and account limits

News. On Take Profit Trader the TEST is unrestricted, but PRO and PRO+ must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI. Tradeify's news policy isn't part of our latest audit, so check its help center before trading releases.

Accounts. TPT: up to 5 active funded accounts per person (PRO and PRO+ combined), unlimited tests, and up to 10 passed-test activations per 30 days; approved copiers only. Tradeify: 5 sim-funded accounts per person and per household, in any mix of plans; copying your own trades is allowed, but opposing positions and copying between people are not.

Tradeify vs Take Profit Trader: quick answers.

Which truly has no daily loss limit? +
Take Profit Trader: none at any size or stage. Tradeify has none on the Select evaluation or the Select Flex path; Growth, Select Daily (funded) and Lightning from 50K have one.
Are both one-time payments? +
No. Tradeify is one-time on every plan (since 03/31/26), with no activation fee. Take Profit Trader bills the TEST monthly ($150 to $360) until you pass, plus a one-time $130 activation fee that promos often waive.
Which is cheaper with code CHAMP? +
TPT only for a one-month pass with the activation fee waived: a 50K TEST is $102 a month after CHAMP's 40%, plus a $130 activation fee unless a promo covers it, against Tradeify Growth's $145 one-time list price before CHAMP, with no activation fee. Take two months and TPT ($204 before activation) costs more. Compare final prices on the deals page.
Which pays out faster? +
Both pay daily on their daily plans: Tradeify Select Daily and every TPT PRO account. Tradeify's other plans pay every 5 days. TPT's wallet transfers on Tradovate PRO take seconds; bank withdrawals take up to 12 business hours.
Which has the better news rule? +
Take Profit Trader's TEST has no news rule; PRO and PRO+ must be flat 1 minute either side of FOMC, NFP and CPI. We haven't verified Tradeify's news policy in our latest audit, so check its help center. More: news trading rules by firm.
