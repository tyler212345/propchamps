Alpha Futures vs Tradeify

Alpha's evaluations are monthly subscriptions; Tradeify's plans are one-time payments. Past the billing, the big differences are Alpha Advanced's $15,000 withdrawals and Tradeify Select's daily payouts.

The bottom line

The short version.

Alpha Futures is built around fewer, larger withdrawals: up to 4 a month, each after 5 winning days of $200 or more, capped at $1,000 to $2,500 on Zero, $3,000 to $5,000 on Standard and $15,000 per request on Advanced. Advanced has no daily loss limit at any stage and no news restrictions, and Zero can pass in a day. Evaluations are monthly until you pass, with $0 activation; Direct is a one-time, straight-to-funded plan. Alpha publishes $70M+ in performance fees paid.

Tradeify charges once and never an activation fee. Its Select evaluation has no daily loss limit; after passing you choose Daily payouts (capped at $600 to $2,500) or Flex (no DLL, capped at $1,250 to $4,500 on a 5-day cycle). Growth has no evaluation consistency rule but a DLL and a 35% funded rule, and Lightning skips the evaluation. Tradeify publishes a $350M+ payout total.

In one line

Want $15,000 withdrawals with no DLL or news limits? Alpha Advanced. Want daily payouts and one-time pricing with no activation fee? Tradeify Select.

  • BillingAlpha Zero, Standard and Advanced: monthly until you pass; Direct one-time. Tradeify: one-time on every plan since 03/31/26.
  • Payout timingAlpha: up to 4 withdrawals a month. Tradeify: Select Daily daily; Growth, Select Flex and Lightning every 5 days.
  • Biggest withdrawalAlpha Advanced: $15,000 per request. Tradeify: up to $5,000 (Growth 150K, from the fourth payout, per Tradeify's help center via search index).
  • No DLLAlpha: Advanced (evaluation and qualified) and Standard (evaluation only). Tradeify: Select evaluation and Select Flex; Lightning at 25K.
  • One-day passAlpha Zero and Tradeify Growth (1-day minimum); no evaluation consistency rule on either.
  • AccountsAlpha: 5 qualified (3 if you hold any Advanced), one person per household. Tradeify: 5 sim-funded per person and household.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: Alpha Zero, Standard, Advanced and Direct vs Tradeify

Both firms sell four ways in, and each has a close counterpart on the other side.

Alpha Zero vs Tradeify Growth

One-day evaluations

Neither checks consistency in the evaluation, and both have a DLL (Zero $500 to $2,000; Growth $600 to $3,750). Funded, Zero checks 40% and Growth 35%. Zero covers 25K to 100K and caps withdrawals at $1,000 to $2,500; Growth covers 25K to 150K, with caps that rise from $1,000 to $2,500 on the first payout to $1,000 to $5,000 by the fourth.

Alpha Advanced vs Tradeify Select Flex

No daily loss limit

Both check 40% in the evaluation and none funded, and both need at least 3 evaluation days. Advanced has no DLL at either stage and no news restrictions, and allows $15,000 per request up to 4 times a month. Select's evaluation has no DLL and neither does the Flex path, which caps each payout at $1,250 to $4,500 every 5 days. Advanced costs $209 to $489 a month; Select is $109 to $369 once.

Alpha Standard vs Tradeify Select Daily

Frequency vs size

Standard checks 50% in the evaluation and 40% qualified, and caps withdrawals at $3,000 to $5,000, up to 4 times a month. Select Daily checks 40% in the evaluation and none funded, and pays daily, capped at $600 to $2,500. Both add a DLL only once funded (Standard $1,000 to $3,000; Select Daily $500 to $1,750).

Alpha Direct vs Tradeify Lightning

No evaluation

The same first profit goal ($1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000), 20% consistency to start (Lightning rises to 25%, then 30%), and the same early caps ($1,000 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000). Alpha Direct's minimum is $500 against Lightning's $1,000, and it can pay up to 4 times a month; Lightning pays every 5 days. Lightning lists lower at every size ($345 to $796 vs $349 to $859), before discounts (at our Oct 23 check, Alpha's public MYALPHA50 took 50% off Direct).

Fine print

News rules, account limits and copy trading

News. Alpha has no news restrictions in any evaluation or on Advanced qualified accounts; on Zero, Standard and Direct qualified accounts, no orders may execute within 2 minutes either side of a red-folder event (you may hold through it). Tradeify's news policy isn't part of our latest audit, so check its help center before trading releases.

Accounts. Alpha: 5 qualified accounts on Zero, Standard (3 if bought before Jul 27, 2026) or Direct, but any active Advanced caps you at 3, and only one individual per household. Tradeify: 5 sim-funded accounts per person and per household, in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning. Both allow copying trades across your own accounts; Tradeify bars opposing positions and copying between people, and Alpha bars automated, group and reverse trading.

Tradeify trading rules. On funded accounts, at least half of your profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds, and you must trade at least once per calendar week.

Frequently asked

Alpha Futures vs Tradeify: quick answers.

Is Alpha Futures or Tradeify better? +
For large, infrequent withdrawals, Alpha Advanced: up to $15,000 per request with no DLL or news restrictions. For frequent payouts and one up-front price, Tradeify: Select Daily pays daily and every plan is one-time. Their one-day-pass and no-evaluation plans (Zero vs Growth, Direct vs Lightning) are close.
Is Alpha Futures monthly or one-time? +
Alpha Zero, Standard and Advanced are monthly subscriptions that stop once you pass; qualified accounts have no monthly fee and activation is $0. Alpha Direct is one-time. Tradeify is one-time on every plan (since 03/31/26).
Which is cheaper, Alpha Futures or Tradeify? +
For a fast pass, Alpha: a 50K Zero is $139 a month list, or $83.40 after 40% off (CHAMP's rate on file; Alpha's public MYALPHA40 also took 40% off Zero at our Oct 23 check), against Tradeify Growth's $145 one-time list price. If you need a second month, Alpha ($166.80) costs more than Growth's list price. Tradeify applies CHAMP at checkout, so check its final price too.
Which has no daily loss limit, Alpha or Tradeify? +
Alpha Advanced has none at either stage, and Alpha Standard has none in the evaluation. Tradeify's Select evaluation and Select Flex path have none, and Lightning has none at 25K. Alpha Zero, Tradeify Growth, Select Daily (funded) and Lightning from 50K all have one.
Which allows bigger payouts, Alpha or Tradeify? +
Alpha Advanced, at up to $15,000 per request. Tradeify's largest cap is $5,000 (Growth 150K, from the fourth payout, per search-index copies of Tradeify's help center in Oct 2026; confirm in your dashboard); Select Flex tops out at $4,500 and Select Daily at $2,500.
How many accounts can you have at Alpha and Tradeify? +
Alpha: 5 qualified accounts on Zero, Standard or Direct, or 3 if you hold any Advanced, with one individual per household. Tradeify: 5 sim-funded accounts per person and per household, in any mix of plans.
