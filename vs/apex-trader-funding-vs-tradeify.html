Apex Trader Funding vs Tradeify

The biggest CHAMP discount against the most payout options. Apex undercuts on entry price and pays you 100% of each approved payout; Tradeify charges once, with no activation fee, and adds daily and no-DLL paths.

The bottom line

The short version.

Apex Trader Funding is the volume play. CHAMP takes 90% off a one-time evaluation that can pass in a day, you can hold up to 20 funded PAs, and PAs keep 100% of each approved payout, every 5 trading days. The trade-offs: a 50% funded consistency rule, a per-payout cap that rises over a maximum of 6 payouts, and either intraday trailing drawdown (Intraday Trail) or a $500 to $2,000 DLL (EOD Trail).

Tradeify trades the rock-bottom entry price for payout options. Every plan is one-time with no activation fee. The Select evaluation has no DLL, and after passing you choose Daily payouts (capped at $600 to $2,500) or Flex (no DLL, capped at $1,250 to $4,500). Growth also passes in a day, with a 35% funded consistency rule. Tradeify pays 90/10 and publishes a $350M+ payout total, against Apex's $800M+.

In one line

Want the cheapest path to funding and plan to scale accounts? Apex. Want daily payouts or no DLL, with one price up front? Tradeify Select.

  • SplitApex: 100% of each approved payout. Tradeify: 90/10.
  • Payout timingApex: every 5 trading days. Tradeify: Select Daily daily; the other plans every 5 days.
  • Payout capsApex: rises to $1,000 to $5,000 by the sixth payout, up to 6 per PA. Tradeify Growth: rises to $1,000 to $5,000 from the fourth; Select Daily $600 to $2,500.
  • Daily loss limitApex Intraday Trail: none in the evaluation; EOD Trail $500 to $2,000. Tradeify: none on the Select evaluation or Select Flex; Growth $600 to $3,750.
  • Funded consistencyApex: 50%. Tradeify: Growth 35%, Select none.
  • AccountsApex: up to 20 PAs. Tradeify: 5 sim-funded per person and household.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: Apex Intraday and EOD Trail vs Tradeify

Apex sells one evaluation in two drawdown styles; Tradeify sells Growth, Select (Daily or Flex after you pass) and Lightning.

Apex vs Tradeify Growth

One-day passes

The same targets at every size ($1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000), and neither checks consistency in the evaluation. Growth has a DLL ($600 to $3,750) and more drawdown at 100K and 150K ($3,500 and $5,000 vs $3,000 and $4,000). Funded, Apex checks 50% and pays 100%; Growth checks 35% and pays 90/10. Both start at the same first-payout caps: $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500.

Apex Intraday Trail vs Tradeify Select

No DLL in the evaluation

Both have no DLL in the evaluation. Intraday Trail trails your intraday high and adds a scaling DLL once funded. Select trails end-of-day, checks 40% consistency and needs 3 days, and its Flex path stays DLL-free when funded.

Apex vs Tradeify Select Daily

Daily payouts

Apex pays every 5 trading days. Select Daily pays daily, capped at $600 to $2,500, with a funded DLL ($500 to $1,750) and no funded consistency rule.

Apex vs Tradeify Lightning

No evaluation

Apex has no straight-to-funded plan. Lightning starts funded ($345 to $796 list, one-time), with 20% to 30% consistency and capped payouts every 5 days.

Getting paid

Payout caps compared

Caps: Apex and Tradeify Growth start at the same first-payout caps ($1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500). Fine print

News rules and account limits

News. Apex allows trading through news with your normal strategy, but not news-only or straddle strategies or hedging, and positions must be flat by the close. Tradeify's news policy isn't part of our latest audit, so check its help center before trading releases.

Accounts. Apex: up to 20 active PAs across a household, with unlimited evaluations. Tradeify: 5 sim-funded accounts per person and per household, in any mix of plans. Tradeify allows copying your own trades across your own accounts (no opposing positions); we couldn't confirm Apex's copy-trading policy.

Frequently asked

Apex Trader Funding vs Tradeify: quick answers.

Is Apex Trader Funding cheaper than Tradeify? +
Usually, with CHAMP: 90% off a 50K Apex Intraday Trail evaluation ($249 list) is $24.90 by our math, or $49 on the No-Activation build. Tradeify Growth 50K lists at $145 before CHAMP, with no activation fee. Apex's Standard build adds a funded activation fee that the coupon doesn't cover.
Which has no daily loss limit, Apex or Tradeify? +
Both have options. Apex Intraday Trail has none in the evaluation (a scaling DLL applies once funded). Tradeify's Select evaluation has none, and its Select Flex path stays DLL-free when funded. Apex EOD Trail and Tradeify Growth have one.
Which pays out faster, Apex or Tradeify? +
On cadence, Tradeify Select Daily (daily). Apex and Tradeify's other plans pay every 5 days. Our estimated median payout times (PropChamps estimates, not firm data) are about 4 hours at Apex and 5 days at Tradeify.
Which is better for news trading? +
Apex allows trading through news with your normal strategy, though not news-only or straddle strategies. We haven't verified Tradeify's news policy in our latest audit, so check its help center. More: news trading rules by firm.
Can I use code CHAMP on both? +
Yes. CHAMP takes 90% off Apex's evaluation in our deal data and is applied at checkout at Tradeify. See current prices on the deals page.
