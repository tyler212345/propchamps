Lucid Trading vs Tradeify

Both pay 90/10, both use end-of-day drawdown on most plans, and each has a daily-payout plan and a no-evaluation plan. The differences are in the daily loss limit, the consistency rules, the payout caps and how you pay.

The bottom line

The short version.

Lucid Trading is the more configurable of the two. The daily loss limit is optional on Pro, Flex and Daily, the Pro evaluation has no consistency rule (so it can be passed in a day), and LucidDaily lets you request a payout every day with no per-payout cap above the buffer. Lucid's evaluations have free activation. The trade-offs: funded Pro accounts check 40% consistency, LucidDaily's funded drawdown is intraday and red-folder news is a hard breach there, and you can hold at most 5 funded accounts per household across every Lucid type.

Tradeify is the simpler buy. Every plan is a one-time payment with no activation fee, and the Select evaluation has no daily loss limit. Once you pass Select you choose Daily payouts (capped at $600 to $2,500 per payout) or Flex (no DLL, capped at $1,250 to $4,500, paid on a 5-day cycle). Growth has no evaluation consistency rule but does have a DLL and a 35% funded rule. Tradeify publishes a $350M+ payout total; we haven't been able to verify Lucid's figure yet.

In one line

Want an optional daily loss limit and daily payouts with no per-payout cap? Lucid. Want one-time pricing with no activation fee and a no-DLL evaluation? Tradeify Select.

  • Daily loss limitLucid: optional on Pro, Flex and Daily; Direct has one from 50K. Tradeify: Growth has one at every size; Select has none in the evaluation or on the Flex path.
  • ConsistencyLucid Pro: none in the evaluation, 40% funded. Tradeify Growth: none in the evaluation, 35% funded. Lucid Flex and Tradeify Select: 40% in the evaluation, none funded.
  • Daily payoutsLucidDaily: no per-payout cap above the buffer. Tradeify Select Daily: $600 to $2,500 per payout.
  • No evaluationLucid Direct and Tradeify Lightning: the same first-payout profit goals and the same caps for payouts 1-3.
  • Price at 50KLucid Pro $115.40 with CHAMP at our last check. Tradeify Growth $145 list, one-time, before CHAMP.
  • PlatformsLucid: Tradovate, NinjaTrader, TradingView, TradeSea, MotiveWave, Quantower and six more CQG/Rithmic platforms. Tradeify: Tradovate, WealthCharts and Tradesea / Rithmic.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: which Lucid account matches which Tradeify account?

Each firm sells four ways in, and they pair up neatly. The cards match them; the tables below give every plan's rules and payouts by size.

Lucid Pro vs Tradeify Growth

1-day-pass evaluations

Neither checks consistency in the evaluation. Pro's DLL is optional (none at 25K); Growth has one at every size ($600 to $3,750). Once funded, Pro checks 40% consistency and Growth 35%. Pro's 25K target is lower ($1,250 vs $1,500); Growth gives more drawdown at 100K and 150K ($3,500 and $5,000 vs $3,000 and $4,500).

Lucid Flex vs Tradeify Select Flex

No consistency rule once funded

Both evaluations check 40% consistency (each firm sells a 50% add-on) and neither checks it once funded. Lucid Flex pays after 5 profit days, 50% of profit up to $1,000 to $3,000, for up to 5 payouts before moving you live. Select Flex has no DLL in the evaluation or when funded and caps each payout at $1,250 to $4,500.

LucidDaily vs Tradeify Select Daily

Daily payouts

LucidDaily lets you request daily once your profit clears the buffer, with no per-payout cap; reaching the max daily sim profit ($6K to $12K) moves you live. Its funded drawdown is intraday. Select Daily caps each payout at $600 to $2,500, adds a funded DLL ($500 to $1,750) and keeps end-of-day drawdown.

Lucid Direct vs Tradeify Lightning

No evaluation

Almost the same design: a $1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000 profit goal before the first payout, 20% consistency to start (Lightning rises to 25%, then 30%), and the same caps for payouts 1-3. Lightning's minimum request is $1,000 against Lucid's $500, and it gives a little more drawdown at 100K and 150K.

Getting paid

Lucid vs Tradeify payout rules and caps

Which pays out faster? On paper, the two daily plans: LucidDaily and Tradeify Select Daily both let you request every day. Lucid's other plans have no fixed payout window (you request once the profit goal, profit days or consistency rule is met), while Tradeify's Growth, Select Flex and Lightning run on a 5-day cycle. Lucid says approved funds are sent within 2 business days. Both firms pay the trader 90%.

Fine print

Account limits, copy trading and platforms

How many accounts? Lucid allows up to 10 evaluations and 5 funded accounts per household, counting every Lucid type together (for example 3 Direct and 2 Pro), and permits trade copiers. Tradeify allows 5 sim-funded accounts per person and per household in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning; copying trades across your own accounts is allowed as long as positions don't oppose each other, and copying between people isn't.

Platforms. Lucid runs on Tradovate, NinjaTrader, TradingView, TradeSea, MotiveWave and Quantower, plus six more CQG and Rithmic platforms. Tradeify runs on Tradovate, WealthCharts and Tradesea / Rithmic.

Trading rules worth knowing. Lucid requires positions flat by 4:45 PM EST, and LucidDaily treats red-folder news as a hard breach on funded accounts. On Tradeify funded accounts, at least half of your profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds, and you must trade at least once per calendar week.

Frequently asked

Lucid Trading vs Tradeify: quick answers.

Is Lucid Trading or Tradeify better? +
It depends on the plan. For a 1-day-pass evaluation, Lucid Pro makes the DLL optional and has a lower 25K target, while Tradeify Growth has the lighter funded consistency rule (35% vs 40%). For daily payouts, LucidDaily has no per-payout cap above the buffer; Tradeify Select Daily caps each payout at $600 to $2,500 but keeps end-of-day drawdown. For no-evaluation accounts, Lucid Direct and Tradeify Lightning are close to identical.
Which is cheaper, Lucid or Tradeify? +
At 50K, Lucid Pro was $115.40 with CHAMP at our last check and Lucid Flex $90.20; Tradeify Growth lists at $145 one-time before CHAMP. Neither charges an activation fee on its evaluations. Tradeify doesn't publish a fixed CHAMP rate, so compare the final prices at checkout or on the deals page.
Which pays out faster, Lucid or Tradeify? +
Both have a daily-payout plan: LucidDaily and Tradeify Select Daily. On their other plans, Lucid has no fixed payout window (you request once you're eligible) and Tradeify runs a 5-day cycle. Lucid says approved funds go out within 2 business days.
Does Lucid or Tradeify have a daily loss limit? +
Lucid's DLL is optional on Pro, Flex and Daily; Direct has one from 50K up. At Tradeify, Growth has a DLL at every size, the Select evaluation and the Select Flex path have none, and Select Daily (funded) and Lightning (50K and up) have one. More in daily loss limits explained.
Lucid Flex vs Tradeify Growth: which is better? +
They suit different traders. Lucid Flex checks 40% consistency in the evaluation and none once funded, and pays 50% of profit (up to $1,000 to $3,000) after 5 profit days. Tradeify Growth has no consistency rule in the evaluation but 35% once funded, a DLL at every size, and caps that rise to $1,000 to $5,000 by the fourth payout.
Tradeify vs Lucid vs Apex: which should I pick? +
Apex Trader Funding pays 100% of each approved payout every 5 trading days and runs the biggest CHAMP discount (90% off), but it closes a funded account after 6 payouts and checks 50% consistency. Compare it directly in Apex vs Lucid Trading and Apex vs Tradeify.
