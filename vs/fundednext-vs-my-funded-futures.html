FundedNext vs My Funded Futures

Two fast-growing, multi-plan futures firms — both now one-time payments. FundedNext leads with the highest reward share on the site; My Funded Futures counters with daily payouts and a ladder to a real live account. Here's how they compare.

The bottom line

The short version.

FundedNext (its futures product) has the standout number: its Flex plan pays a 95% reward share — the highest we track — on a 5-benchmark-day cycle. It runs Flex, Legacy, and two Rapid paths (Pro and Daily), all on end-of-day trailing drawdown, all one-time payments, with a 40% consistency rule by phase.

My Funded Futures is built around speed and a path to the real thing. Its Rapid plan pays daily and switches to intraday trailing once funded (the drawdown locks after a $100 buffer); Builder gives you a five-payout ladder to a live funded account at Blue Row Capital. MFF is also now one-time across the board, with no daily loss limit and no activation fee.

In one line

Want the highest reward share (95% on Flex)? FundedNext. Want daily payouts and a ladder to a real live account? My Funded Futures.

Frequently asked

FundedNext vs My Funded Futures — quick answers.

Which pays a bigger share of your profit? +
FundedNext — its Flex plan pays a 95% reward share, the highest we track. My Funded Futures pays 90/10 on Rapid and 80/20 on Pro. Match the plan to your goal in the comparison above.
Which pays out faster? +
My Funded Futures Rapid pays daily. FundedNext's Rapid Daily also pays daily, but its flagship Flex pays on a 5-benchmark-day cycle. Both process quickly.
Are both one-time payments now? +
Yes. Both FundedNext and My Funded Futures moved to one-time purchases (no monthly renewals). Confirm the current promo price for each at checkout.
How do their drawdowns differ? +
FundedNext uses end-of-day trailing drawdown on every plan. My Funded Futures Rapid uses EOD drawdown in the eval, then switches to intraday trailing once funded — which locks permanently after a $100 buffer.
