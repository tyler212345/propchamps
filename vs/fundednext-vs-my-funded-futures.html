FundedNext Futures vs My Funded Futures

Both are one-time, multi-plan futures firms with no activation fee. FundedNext's Flex pays a 95% split; My Funded Futures counters with daily payouts on Rapid and a ladder to a real live account. Here's how they compare.

The bottom line

The short version.

FundedNext Futures has the standout split: Flex pays 95%, the highest we track after Apex's 100%, with no daily loss limit and no buffer, but caps each withdrawal at 50% of profit ($1,500 / $2,500 / $4,000) and ends after 5 rewards. Every FundedNext futures account trails end-of-day, news trading is allowed on all of them, and Rapid Daily has no consistency rule at either stage. The $380M+ it publishes covers all FundedNext programs, not just futures.

My Funded Futures is built around speed and a path to the real thing. Rapid pays daily at 90/10 once you clear the buffer, with no stated per-payout cap, but switches to intraday trailing drawdown once funded (it stops trailing at +$100). Builder's five approved sim payouts unlock a real live account at Blue Row Capital. There's no daily loss limit on Rapid, Rapid EOD or Pro (Builder has a soft pause), and MFF bans Tier-1 news on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts.

In one line

Want the highest split (95% on Flex) and news trading? FundedNext. Want daily payouts with no stated cap and a ladder to a real live account? My Funded Futures.

  • SplitFundedNext: Flex 95%, Rapid 90%, Legacy 80%. MFF: 90/10 on Rapid and Rapid EOD, 80/20 on Pro and Builder.
  • Daily payoutsFundedNext Rapid Daily: $800 to $2,500 per payout. MFF Rapid and Rapid EOD: no stated cap.
  • Funded drawdownFundedNext: end-of-day on every plan. MFF Rapid: intraday until it locks at +$100; the other MFF plans end-of-day.
  • NewsFundedNext: allowed on every account. MFF: no Tier-1 news on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts.
  • Account lifeFundedNext Flex and Rapid end after 5 rewards (Legacy doesn't). MFF Builder leads to a live account after 5 payouts.
  • SizesFundedNext: up to 150K (Flex) or 100K (Legacy, Rapid). MFF: up to 150K on Rapid, Pro and Builder.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: FundedNext Flex, Legacy and Rapid vs MFF

FundedNext Futures sells Flex, Legacy and two Rapid versions; MFF sells Rapid, Rapid EOD, Pro and Builder. These are the closest matches.

FundedNext Rapid Daily vs MFF Rapid

Daily payouts

Rapid Daily passes in a day with no consistency rule at either stage and trails end-of-day, but has a DLL ($500 / $1,000 / $1,250) and caps payouts at $800 / $1,200 / $2,500; it ends after 5 rewards. MFF Rapid needs 2 days and 50% consistency to pass, then has no funded consistency, no DLL and no stated cap, with daily payouts once the buffer is cleared, but its funded drawdown trails intraday.

FundedNext Flex vs MFF Rapid EOD

End-of-day, no DLL

Both trail end-of-day when funded and neither has a DLL. Flex pays 95% (50% of profit up to $1,500 to $4,000, after 5 benchmark days) and covers 50K to 150K. Rapid EOD pays 90/10 daily with no cap per payout, but only at 25K and 50K, after a 4-day, 30%-consistency evaluation.

FundedNext Rapid Pro vs MFF Builder

One-day passes

Neither checks consistency in the evaluation. Rapid Pro checks 40% once funded, pays 90% every 3 days, caps at $800 to $2,500 and has no DLL. Builder checks 50% at the payout stage, pays 80/20 with a first request 48 hours after your first trade, caps each cycle at $1,000 to $4,500 and has a soft-pause DLL; 5 approved payouts unlock a live account.

FundedNext Legacy vs MFF Pro

The long-runners

Legacy (80%, 25K to 100K) removes its 50% limit and cap after 30 benchmark days and doesn't end after 5 rewards. MFF Pro (80/20, 50K to 150K) pays every 14 calendar days with a $1,000 minimum, allows up to $100K in sim payouts per user, and reviews you for Live after 3 consecutive payouts or $20K profit.

Getting paid

FundedNext vs MFF payout rules and caps

Which pays out faster? Both have daily plans: FundedNext Rapid Daily, and MFF Rapid and Rapid EOD. MFF's first Rapid payout can come 24 hours after your first funded trade, and MFF says most payouts are approved instantly (manual reviews take 6 to 12 business hours). FundedNext says about 99.99% of payouts are processed within 24 hours. MFF Pro is the slow plan, paying every 14 calendar days.

Fine print

News trading and account limits

News. FundedNext allows news trading on every futures account. MFF bans Tier-1 news (FOMC, NFP, CPI and similar) on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after; it's allowed in every evaluation and on Builder.

Accounts. FundedNext: up to 5 active funded accounts per user and per household, with at most 10 issued per calendar month. MFF: up to 10 evaluations; 5 sim-funded accounts if all are 25K or 50K, 3 if any is 100K or 150K; Builder is limited to 1 sim-funded account per user (2 at 25K). Both allow copying trades across your own accounts.

Frequently asked

FundedNext Futures vs My Funded Futures: quick answers.

Which pays a bigger share of your profit? +
FundedNext: Flex pays 95%, Rapid 90% and Legacy 80%. My Funded Futures pays 90/10 on Rapid and Rapid EOD and 80/20 on Pro and Builder.
Which pays out faster? +
Both have daily plans (FundedNext Rapid Daily; MFF Rapid and Rapid EOD). MFF says most payouts are approved instantly, and Rapid's first can come 24 hours after your first funded trade; FundedNext says about 99.99% are processed within 24 hours. FundedNext Flex pays on a 5-benchmark-day cycle.
Are both one-time payments? +
Yes. FundedNext Futures charges a one-time fee, and MFF has been one-time on every plan since August 2026. Neither charges an activation fee.
How do their drawdowns differ? +
FundedNext trails end-of-day on every plan. MFF Rapid trails end-of-day in the evaluation, then intraday once funded until the max-loss level reaches +$100, where it stops. MFF Rapid EOD, Pro and Builder stay end-of-day.
Can you trade news on FundedNext or MFF? +
FundedNext: yes, on every futures account. MFF: not Tier-1 news on funded Rapid, Rapid EOD or Pro accounts (2 minutes either side); yes in evaluations and on Builder. See news trading rules by firm.
Which has the higher payout cap? +
MFF. Rapid has no stated per-payout cap, Rapid EOD says "no cap", and Pro allows up to $100K in sim payouts per user. FundedNext caps Flex at 50% of profit up to $4,000, Legacy at $6,000 (until 30 benchmark days) and Rapid at $2,500.
