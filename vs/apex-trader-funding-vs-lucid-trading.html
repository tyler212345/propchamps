Apex Trader Funding vs Lucid Trading

Apex keeps none of your approved payouts and runs the biggest CHAMP discount; Lucid pays 90/10 across four plan types, with an optional daily loss limit and a daily-payout plan. Here's how they compare, plan by plan.

The bottom line

The short version.

Apex Trader Funding is the cheapest way in on paper: CHAMP takes 90% off the evaluation, which is a one-time fee with no rebill. Funded accounts (PAs) keep 100% of each approved payout, paid every 5 trading days, with a 50% consistency rule and a per-payout cap that rises over a maximum of 6 payouts. You choose Intraday Trail (no DLL in the evaluation) or EOD Trail (end-of-day drawdown with a $500 to $2,000 DLL); both can pass in a day.

Lucid Trading pays 90/10 but gives you more ways to get paid: Pro and Direct on request once you're eligible (no fixed window), Flex after 5 profit days, and LucidDaily every day with no per-payout cap above the buffer. Pro, Flex and Direct use end-of-day drawdown, the DLL is optional on Pro, Flex and Daily, and the evaluations have free activation. Where Apex closes a PA after 6 payouts, payout 5 puts Lucid Pro and Direct accounts in the live review pool.

In one line

Want the biggest discount and a 100% payout, and fine with 50% consistency and a 6-payout limit? Apex. Want end-of-day drawdown, an optional DLL and daily payouts? Lucid.

  • SplitApex: 100% of each approved payout. Lucid: 90/10 on every plan.
  • Payout timingApex: every 5 trading days. Lucid: on request (Pro, Direct), after 5 profit days (Flex) or daily (LucidDaily).
  • Funded consistencyApex: 50%. Lucid: Pro 40%, Direct 20%, Flex and Daily none.
  • DrawdownApex: Intraday Trail or EOD Trail, $1,000 to $4,000. Lucid: end-of-day on Pro, Flex and Direct ($1,000 to $4,500 on Pro).
  • Payouts per accountApex: up to 6 per PA, then it closes. Lucid: payout 5 puts Pro and Direct accounts in the live review pool.
  • Price at 50KApex Intraday Trail: $249 list, 90% off with CHAMP. Lucid Pro: $115.40 with CHAMP at our last check.

Plan by plan

Plan by plan: Apex Intraday and EOD Trail vs Lucid

Apex sells one evaluation in two drawdown styles, each as a Standard build (plus a funded activation fee after you pass) or a No-Activation-Fee build (no activation fee). Lucid sells four plan types. These are the matchups that matter.

Apex vs Lucid Pro

1-day-pass evaluations

The same 50K target ($3,000) and drawdown ($2,000), and neither checks consistency in the evaluation. Apex's 25K target is higher ($1,500 vs $1,250) and its 150K drawdown lower ($4,000 vs $4,500). Funded, Apex checks 50% consistency and pays 100%; Lucid Pro checks 40% and pays 90/10. First-payout caps: Apex $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500, Lucid Pro $1,000 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000.

Intraday or EOD Trail vs Lucid's optional DLL

The daily loss limit

Apex makes you choose up front: Intraday Trail has no DLL in the evaluation (a scaling DLL applies once funded) but trails your intraday high; EOD Trail only moves at the close but adds a $500 to $2,000 DLL. Lucid Pro trails end-of-day and lets you switch the DLL on or off at purchase; above the initial trail, the funded DLL scales to 60% of your highest end-of-day profit (LucidScale).

Apex vs LucidDaily

Daily payouts

Apex pays every 5 trading days, with 5 qualifying days per payout. LucidDaily lets you request every day once your profit clears the buffer, with no per-payout cap; its funded drawdown is intraday, and red-folder news is a hard breach there.

Apex vs Lucid Direct

No evaluation

Apex has no straight-to-funded plan. Lucid Direct starts funded for a one-time price ($230.30 to $585.20 with CHAMP at our last check), with 20% consistency and capped payouts.

Frequently asked

Apex Trader Funding vs Lucid Trading: quick answers.

Which is cheaper, Apex or Lucid? +
Apex, on list price with CHAMP: 90% off a 50K Intraday Trail evaluation ($249 list) is $24.90 by our math, or $49 on the No-Activation build. Lucid Pro 50K was $115.40 with CHAMP at our last check. Apex's Standard build adds a funded activation fee after you pass, which the coupon doesn't cover; Lucid's evaluations have free activation. Confirm both on the deals page.
Which lets you keep more of your profit? +
Apex pays 100% of each approved payout; Lucid pays 90%. But Apex caps each payout on a rising ladder, closes a PA after 6 payouts and checks 50% consistency, while Lucid's caps and rules vary by plan and LucidDaily has no per-payout cap above the buffer.
Which pays out faster, Apex or Lucid? +
LucidDaily lets you request every day. Apex pays every 5 trading days. Lucid Pro and Direct have no fixed window, and Lucid sends approved funds within 2 business days.
How do their drawdowns differ? +
Apex makes you pick Intraday Trail (no DLL in the evaluation) or EOD Trail (with a $500 to $2,000 DLL), $1,000 to $4,000 by size. Lucid trails end-of-day on Pro, Flex and Direct; LucidDaily lets you choose in the evaluation and trails intraday once funded. More: Apex trailing drawdown explained.
Can you trade news on Apex or Lucid? +
Apex allows trading through news with your normal strategy, but not news-only or straddle strategies or hedging, and positions must be flat by the close. Lucid allows news on Pro, Flex and Direct; LucidDaily makes red-folder news a hard breach once funded. See news trading rules by firm.
Apex vs Lucid vs Tradeify: which should I pick? +
Tradeify sits between them: one-time pricing with no activation fee, 90/10 like Lucid, a Select evaluation with no DLL, and daily payouts on Select Daily (capped at $600 to $2,500). Compare them in Lucid Trading vs Tradeify and Apex vs Tradeify.
Which Lucid account is closest to Apex? +
Lucid Pro: a 1-day-pass evaluation with no evaluation consistency rule, 40% once funded. If you would rather skip the evaluation, Lucid Direct; for daily payouts, LucidDaily. The Lucid Trading review covers all four.
