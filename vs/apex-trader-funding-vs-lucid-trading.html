Apex Trader Funding vs Lucid Trading

Two of the biggest discount-driven futures firms. Apex leans on the deepest discount and now keeps 100% of your payout; Lucid trades a split for four flexible paths and a configurable evaluation. Here's how they compare.

Jump to The verdict Full comparison Which to pick FAQ
The bottom line

The short version.

Apex Trader Funding is the discount-and-volume heavyweight: CHAMP takes 90% off the evaluation, funded accounts now keep a flat 100% of the payout (no split), and it has by far the deeper track record ($800M+ paid). You pick one of two trailing types — Intraday Trail (tighter, no eval daily loss limit) or EOD Trail (end-of-day drawdown with a DLL) — and pass in a single day.

Lucid Trading keeps a 90/10 split but gives you more room to shape the account: four paths (Pro, Flex, Daily, Direct), an optional daily-loss-limit toggle, and a configurable Daily eval where you pick EOD or intraday drawdown. Its funded accounts use the LucidScale model (the daily loss limit above your initial trail becomes 60% of your peak end-of-day balance). Both firms cap payouts per request in the sim-funded stage until you reach the live stage.

In one line

Want the biggest discount, a 100% payout with no split, and the longest payout history? Apex. Want path flexibility, an optional DLL, and a configurable eval? Lucid.

Frequently asked

Apex Trader Funding vs Lucid Trading — quick answers.

Which is cheaper — Apex or Lucid? +
Both run large CHAMP discounts, but Apex advertises the single biggest one — 90% off the evaluation. Lucid discounts at checkout too (the exact rate varies), so confirm both current prices on the deals page.
Which lets you keep more of your profit? +
Apex now pays a flat 100% with no split in the sim-funded stage. Lucid keeps a 90/10 split (you keep 90%). Note Apex caps each payout on a rising ladder, so read the payout rows above.
How do their drawdowns differ? +
Apex offers a tighter Intraday Trail (no daily loss limit in the eval) or an EOD Trail with a DLL. Lucid uses EOD drawdown with an optional DLL, then the LucidScale model once funded (the DLL above your initial trail becomes 60% of your peak EOD balance).
Do both cap your payouts? +
Yes — in the sim-funded stage. Apex caps each payout on a six-payout ladder (then the account closes); Lucid caps per request until you reach the live stage (LucidDaily is the exception). The exact caps are in the comparison above.
